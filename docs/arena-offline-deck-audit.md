# Offline Masters MVP: deck audit and blocker map

Issue #649 (Arena O4-01), plan `docs/arena-offline-plan.md` section O4. Audited on 13 Oct 2026 against `main` at
`613b0d93` (after #647). Nothing here touches a database, a provider or a live write; the card text comes from the
public deckplanet feed, saved as a 41-card snapshot, and every number below is regenerated by
`scripts/arena-deck-audit.mts` (section 10).

**This document selects candidates and maps blockers. It does not qualify either deck.** A deck is supported only when
its blockers are resolved and its scenario gates pass (plan, "Delivery and pickup rules"); neither is true yet.

- **Owner's own decks are not in this audit.** They live in the database, which agents do not read. The two lists here

## 2. Method and limits

1. **Decklists:** the two lists are entries of `scripts/import-starter-decks.mts` (the repository's own record of four
   Masters starter decks and where each quantity came from). The audit script carries a copy of both entry lists;
   `--check` fails if the importer's text ever stops containing every entry. Nothing was invented or corrected.
2. **Card text:** `fetchDeckplanet("dbs")` + `shapeCatalog` (read-only GETs of
   `https://api.deckplanet.net/cardsearch/dbs_masters_cards`, 6,552 raw records in pages of up to 500) on 13 Oct 2026, then only the 41
   cards of these two decks saved to `docs/arena-backlog/evidence/649/catalog-subset-2026-10-13.json` (not a catalog
   dump). `--live` re-fetches and reports drift against the snapshot.
3. **Legality:** the app's own `legality(rows, "dbs")` from `src/lib/decks/legality.ts`.
4. **Compiler:** `compileCard` from `src/lib/arena/compile` on each face, **plus** the three stages after compiling
   that `scripts/arena-gaps.mts` also measures, because compiling alone proves none of them: `triggersOf` for every
   [Auto] (a compiled Auto with no trigger never fires), the price readers (`costIsOnlyOrbs`, `compileCostProgram`,
   `priceCondition`) for every Activate/Counter/Auto price, and the static layer's `emitsStatic` for every [Permanent].
5. **Runtime:** the same cards are compiled into a replay bundle and played by the local policy (#644-#647; since #650
   `localPolicy()`, which answers every window and interrupts on a question it cannot read, where the #649 run used
   `localPolicy(uniformPolicy)`, uniform for the constrained choices) on the rules-only engine through `playDuel`, recording every prompt kind, every manual skill on the legal list, and
   every skill the engine refuses as `unread` (`RULES_ENGINE.rejectedActions`). Fresh compile, not stored records.
6. **Support tiers** (compilation alone is never support):

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| E | a `supported` row of `CAPABILITY_EVIDENCE` whose own fixture covers the shape |
| K | a named verify scenario plays the keyword on the rules engine |
| D | deck-building rule only; `legality()` enforces it |
| P | a scenario exists but the glossary records a known difference |
| C | compiled only; generic suites may mention the op, but no scenario plays the audited wording |
| B | blocked: unread clause, unreadable price, an Auto with no trigger, an inert Permanent, or refused `unread` by the engine |
| S | structural (the skill's kind); not graded alone |

A skill's tier is its worst mechanic; "evidence" in the skill table means every mechanic is E, K, D or S.

**Not available to this audit (say so, do not guess):** the owner's database (`card_rules` rows with their reviewed
status and stored WHEN/price, hand-entered `cards.specified_cost`, the owner's own decks, `app_users` data), the official
Bandai ban list beyond the `limitedTo`/`isBanned`/`isLimited` flags in the feed, artwork reachability, and any
physical-device or browser run.

## 3. Candidate selection

The importer carries four Masters lists. All four were resolved against the live feed and compiled
(`npx tsx scripts/arena-deck-audit.mts --live --candidates`):

| Deck | Resolves | Main | `legality()` | Cards with an unread skill (copies) | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A SD15 Pride of the Saiyans | yes, by card numbers only | 50 | as printed: illegal (1) + 2 warnings; owner-amended: legal + 2 warnings | 2 (3) | **chosen** (owner-amended) |
| B SD5 The Crimson Saiyan | yes, every name unique | 50 | legal | 0 | **chosen** |
| C SD13 Clan Collusion | yes | 50 | legal + 3 warnings | 4 (7): SD13-02, SD13-04, BT10-080, BT1-107 | first reserve |
| D SD2 The Extreme Evolution | **no**: "Pan" matches 7 cards | 48 | incomplete | 3 (7) | rejected, cannot be resolved without a guess |

Why A and B:

- **B** is the only list that resolves without any ambiguity, is legal, and has no unread text. It is the clean
  baseline and exercises [Swap], [Barrier], [Super Combo], a [Counter: Attack], a [Blocker], Life-card prices and an
  [Awaken] Leader.
- **A** is the only list given entirely as card numbers, from Bandai's own table, and its Leader SD15-01 is in the
  owner's own decks, as is B's SD5-01 (`docs/arena-backlog/s2-98-attack-target-triggers.md`,
  `docs/arena-history-lessons.md`). It brings
  what B lacks: Unison cards and markers, [Spirit Boost], combo Autos, Extras, three Counters, a second colour family
  (red, with a blue and a green off-colour card).
- Together they cover both colours, both Leader shapes, and the two largest mechanic families in the pool. Both are
  mono-colour Saiyan decks, so diversity is low; C is the next pair member if a legal list is required.
- **Owner decision (13 Oct 2026):** A is not legal as printed; the starter list predates the restriction of BT10-008 to
  one copy. The owner amended it: one BT10-008 is dropped (x2 -> x1, limit 1) and a third BT5-023 is added (still 50
  cards). The audit and the pack carry this variant (`DeckSpec.amended` in `scripts/arena-deck-audit.mts`); the importer
  keeps the printed starter and `--check` still requires the printed quantities there.
- The comparison used compile and legality only; C and D were not played.

Reading it:

- **SD15-01's [Awaken] is never offered.** The engine refuses it as `unread` in every game that includes Deck A (60 of
  60). The compiled record has no price
  condition (`price.condition: null`): the printed "life at 4 or less **or** you have a red Unison Card with a specified
  cost of 2" was dropped, so the engine holds the line as unread. The Leader therefore never flips, and its awakened
  back face (including the unread Auto) is unreachable. SD5-01's Awaken, by contrast, is on the legal list in 58 of the 60
  games that include Deck B and awakens in 56.
- **BT5-023, BT6-026 and BT10-008 [Counter] lines are offered since #716** (U5). Before it, BT5-023 and BT6-026 were
  refused `unread` in 32 and 27 games while held in hand (any [Counter] whose printed price was more than orbs was
  refused), and BT10-008's restriction price did not read. Now the leading "Your Leader Card is red" condition is checked
  at offer and declaration, and BT10-008's "You can't activate the [Counter: Play] skills of other cards for the turn" is
  a price put in force before the skill activates. The counts are in the offered list above (`#counter` keys). That
  shows the lines are offered, not that their effects are right (U1).
- **BT4-079's [Swap] is offered since #729** (U4). Before it, its action price ("{y}, choose 1 card from your hand and
  place it in your Drop Area") did not compile and the line was refused `unread` (17 games before #728, 20 after); now
  it is never refused, and the offered list above counts the games it was on the legal list. Every [Swap] now reveals
  only the specified ≪Goku's Lineage≫ card at its cost (#731, U6), so the `[Swap]` choice counts above are of fewer,
  printed-legal candidates than before.
- Nothing else was refused, nothing stopped a game, and `optionalCost`, `replaceMove`, `offering` and `empowerCarry` never
  arose. Auto prices are asked as a `chooseMode` ("Pay this Auto skill's cost?" / "Don't pay"), not as `optionalCost`.
- "Offered" and "reached" show a skill was available, not that its effect was right. `SD15-02` was played as a Unison
  although the feed gives it no cost (U7).
- Cards that never appear in the offered or reached lists in 80 games (BT6-010 reached in 24; TB1-055 in 9; BT9-031 in 17)
  are thin evidence, not evidence of a gap; a staged scenario (U1) is the right tool.
