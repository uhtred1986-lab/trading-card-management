    Motion catalogue

    What we take from Pokémon TCG Pocket

    • One big card per sideThe active card sits in the middle, large; everything else is smaller around it. Here that is the Leader.
    • The number sits on the card's cornerHP (here: power) is a large stroked figure at the top-right of every card, readable without zooming.
    • Zoom is the full printed cardHold any card and the real card fills most of the screen: picture on top, rules below. The board stays visible behind it.
    • Act from the cardA Pokémon's attacks are rows on its card that you tap. Here a usable skill glows on the card as a button, and Attack sits right under it.
    • The opponent's play is shown large firstTheir card appears big in the centre before it moves to its place, so you never miss it.
    • Valid targets glow, the rest is plainWhen something needs a target, only the legal cards light up.
    • No sentences on the boardTurn, legal moves and who is ahead are shown by colour, glow and numbers. Words appear only as printed card rules.
    • Refusals are a short toast"Unable to…" appears at the top in one line; nothing else changes.
    • End Turn sits beside your active cardOn the right edge, at thumb height, away from the hand.
    • Blur what is not the pointTurn changes, a zoomed card, the opponent's play and skills all appear over a blurred board, so the one thing to read stands out. While you pick a target, nothing is blurred.
    • Few things on screenDeck and discard are small counters on the side. Everything else opens on demand.
