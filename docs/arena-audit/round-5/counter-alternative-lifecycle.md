# Alternative Counter payment lifecycle, R461

R460 exposed an older lifecycle defect: an action alternative moved the Counter to Drop, spent its use and fired skillActivated before asking for its payment cards. Its effect alone waited behind a saveVarsAs price frame. Activation Autos could already be pending even though the alternative had not been completed. A native regression failed at the unpaid source already being in Drop.

RM 12-2-2-1/2 requires revealing an Extra, paying its energy cost (including an alternative), then placing it in Drop. Activation follows the required payment (12-2-2-4, 22-10-4); incomplete payment cannot produce activation (5-8-4). The alternative program now uses the existing strict CounterCost continuation. Isolated offer/application checks execute the actual alternative, including original movement results. Complete payment alone moves the source, spends usage, emits activation and opens the normal Counter:Counter response. The original hand occurrence is retained across saved choices and is publicly inspectable while paying, as required by declaration; other hand cards remain hidden. Native snapshots expose only this source through optional SideView.revealedHand; the ordinary opponent hand stays null, hidden payer IDs remain aliased, and the optional field disappears when the occurrence changes or payment completes. A departed/reentered source cannot activate a fresh occurrence; earlier payments remain spent.

Old price frames with exact counter saveVarsAs costvars provenance require rebuilding because they already announced activation. Ordinary unrelated price frames are unchanged. Existing explicit unsupported combinations with separate action/resource costs and activation taxes remain refused; this is not a new composition claim. The historical paid/free event flag remains unchanged, pending separate review.

This runtime correction affects more records than the representative native fixture. No explicit self movement occurs in those programs. Most alternatives choose nonhand cards/markers or explicitly other hand cards; BT13-030 and BT21-097 instead print generic hand discards. That source-specific question remains follow-up work; this receipt does not relabel their print as “other.” BT21-065's specified Mr. Buu payer differs from its Extra source.
