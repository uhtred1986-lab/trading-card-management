# Ordinary Activate action-price completion — R515

The actual full BT18-038 Main skill previously spent its shared Limit before its two-card action price finished. A replacement sending the second discard to the same Drop or to Warp still activated it. A late prohibition did cancel the effect, but left the Limit spent. The read-only baseline exercised both seats, accepted/declined same-Drop and Warp replacements, and a clearly labeled late-prohibition intervention: 10 cases. Clearing only the Limit ledger made the otherwise-payable second copy available.

The ordinary printed action-price producer now binds its original source, full stored rule, price/effect pair, activation moment and shared Limit identity before resources and Extra staging. Its dedicated host reads the existing authoritative original movement callback; a replacement reaching the same area is not original payment. KO costs retain their existing required Drop destination and additionally distinguish original KO from a skill substitute. This does not change generic MovementOutcome, KO event admission, token/Z routing or the independent R506 conditional-KO policy.

A failed payment drops only its paired effect. Earlier resources stay spent, and a prepared replacement child retains its own history and saved questions. The interpreter suspends the original price behind that actual child; it does not reconstruct a substitute from its operations. Only complete payment commits usage, publishes the existing captured skillActivated moment and logs the skill. The previous early publication is suppressed, so this is one moved event, not an added event. A completion-time ceiling check also prevents a saved pending price from exceeding already-consumed Limit or same-lifetime Once.

The optional ordinaryUseGeneration follows exactly the existing native usage reset sites: nonretained departures, ordinary under placement and turn reset increment an initialized generation. Retained movement, control transfer, covered-Leader retention and normal flip keep it. Once and marker-skill eligibility commit only to that same physical usage lifetime; shared Limit uses the original card/face key independently of source departure. Missing or malformed saved generations refuse. This field is absent from both public board views.

The native adapter authenticates the captured full original stored face/skill and chronology. The pure contract accepts only its supported interpreter lowering of discard/reveal/combo programs; it does not assume raw operations remain byte-identical after lowering. Price/effect counterpart uniqueness is checked. Missing old price bindings require rebuilding. Copied ordinary action prices explicitly refuse in the menu and before resources; waived actions and dedicated Awaken, Auto, Counter, Field, Drop and under-Extra runners keep their existing paths. Energy-paid X retains precedence over action-price X. Generic self-reference lifetime behavior is not broadened by this change.

The exact declaration also persists privately in energy-payment and pooled-resource prompts. Resume validates original current occurrence, face and generation before charging and passes it through a private one-hop replay field; it is not reconstructed from a post-resource area or placed in action logs. Both-seat native energy-prompt success/replay, missing-binding and ABA refusals are covered. A last-marker Unison may legitimately leave during resource payment; its subsequent price retains the original Unison declaration rather than becoming a Drop activation. Ordinary DBS lines currently have no selectable Z-Energy pool (keywords use their dedicated path), so pooled coverage is explicitly a direct validation mechanism plus existing dedicated Z-Energy tests, not claimed printed ordinary pooled gameplay.
