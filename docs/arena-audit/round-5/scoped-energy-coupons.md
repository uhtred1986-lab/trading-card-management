# Activation-scoped card-energy coupons (R448)

P-524 and EX25-13 now retain the printed activation scope in a typed energyActivation field. P-524's own FAQ explicitly excludes Activate:Main/Battle from its Activate:Main discount and includes blue Field Extras. Exact Main, Battle and Main/Battle scopes remain distinct; an unqualified Activate scope excludes Counter. R201's existing ordinary skill-cost timing semantics are unchanged. R448 supersedes R447's temporary compiler refusal.

The discount applies only while computing the matching Extra activation payment. Ordinary current card attributes, Counter energy prices and unrelated skill prices retain their previous values. The pending selector can match a card that enters hand after the coupon resolves, using the existing onCard protection semantics. No new immunity or Barrier interpretation is introduced.

Matching one-use coupon IDs are captured at the resource-payment boundary while the Extra is still in its original area. No-action-price activations consume them at successful activation, including zero-energy payments. Ordinary saveVars action prices and strict marker-price continuations consume only after complete payment; failed or declined prices keep the coupon. Expired or missing effects are never restored. Saved captures reject malformed IDs and duplicates. New cost effects retain typed creation provenance, distinguishing a valid ordinary discount from another line or face of the same source. Active frames retain a multiset of complete typed coupon operations, including ordinary operations, so otherwise-identical Main, Battle and unscoped coupons remain distinguishable. Ambiguous old broad coupons and active frames from the affected printed family require rebuilding; stale stored rows remain blocked in every status. This is a bounded migration contract for the identified printed family, not a claim that every historical copied or granted effect has been audited.
