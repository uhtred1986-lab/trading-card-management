## Native historical movement prerequisite

End-to-end tests exposed three preexisting errors hidden by aggregate draw totals. The optional host's actual skill-attributed departure pended a self removal Auto, then the interpreter's legacy alias pended it again. A watched 15000+ power departure failed because its source had 19000 power in Battle but only 10000 after cleanup. A self Energy-arrival Auto incorrectly fired after whole/individual source negation disappeared at departure.

The prerequisite fix is bounded to native version-2 Activate/Auto script Battle-to-Energy moves, including a suppressed/absent optional descriptor. The original mover supplies actor/source attribution independently of the departing card's original controller. Charge, payment and specialized replacement frames are excluded; absent provenance is not manufactured. The host passes captured old Battle attributes through the actual movement event. Watched subject conditions use those attributes when deciding whether to pend, then the existing saved subjectInfo carries them into the pending body. A nonmatching Once-per-turn skill does not consume its use. Self placedInEnergy honors captured original Auto negation and original holder, alongside the existing departure aliases.

The actual movement broadcasts once. A synchronous host-local receipt records the moved id and actual new epoch after successful Battle-to-Energy completion, even when no watcher matched. It suppresses each exact already-broadcast legacy departure alias once. This sequence is synchronous in script.ts: after h.move returns, the original optional child is cleared and the legacy departure calls execute without any ask/wait/save boundary. The receipt is neither stored in VmState nor inferred from the pending queue. A host-local nativeMove wrapper resets it before every subsequent native move attempt; host disposal also ends it. A genuine later reentry/departure is a new event. The native mover still owns all event emission; shared cleanup itself pends nothing.

This is not a global repair of every movement producer lacking historical information. Other movers/destinations that do not supply old attributes remain outside this proof. A separate future gap remains: RM 9-6-9-2 covers original negation in any area, but the host currently captures sourceNegated only for Battle departures. R486's whole-negated Combo fixture had no self Energy-arrival Auto and therefore did not prove that interaction. R489 does not expand to Combo or claim it fixed.
