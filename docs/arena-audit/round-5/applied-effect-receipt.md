## Private receipt contract

A native execution starts an empty receipt at its actual root construction. Its first actual returned continuous-effect ID becomes its anchor; no serial or Counter timing scope is allocated for receipt identity. Every recorded application binds that returned ID, immutable source/root facts and the target's original physical occurrence. A separate private `VmState.appliedEffectOwnership` map binds each live ID to its immutable application and authoritative current occurrence. ContinuousEffect objects and their already-emitted event payloads receive no new fields.

Choices, branches and saved resumes retain the same receipt. Fresh activations (including invoked activations), Autos, Counters, delayed bodies, hooks, taxes and replacement frames do not inherit a parked execution's receipt. An Auto/Counter payment uses a separate payment receipt while its body receipt stays dormant. The strict action-price proof uses a cloned frame/state, so feasibility evaluation cannot mutate live receipts. Pure Unique/Field rule interruptions are not labeled payment roots. A replacement Auto initializes its actual execution when activated, rather than adopting an interrupted body.

Only exact returned card-target effects produced by `vmHost.addEffect`, `negateAll` and `negateSkillIndex` are recorded. The production adapter records actual cards, including an explicit under/removed location marker when no area contains the card. That marker is a location fact, not admission of a new under-card procedure. Player/global effects are outside card-occurrence receipts. Direct native `resolvePlay.beforeTriggers` negation, fresh token negation and the optional Energy-entry Exhaust stamp keep their existing dedicated lifetimes and are deliberately unowned by the parked mover. The Energy payer child belongs to its incoming Permanent; this patch neither treats it as payment of the mover nor assigns the final stamp to the mover. This is not a claim to wire every special native effect producer.

The host and flow movers capture before the synchronous actual move, then update metadata after their unchanged cleanup. Actual area/holder results govern the update, including redirects. Area-pair carry updates **every** retained tagged effect, including a parked parent's effect moved by its child. Same-execution surviving effects retain their current occurrence for the qualifying destination family; this preserves provenance only, including payment provenance, and does not decide a future payment carry entitlement. Nonqualifying resets retire ownership, deleted effects stay deleted, and mutation of an effect's value does not recreate its original value. Retargeted stack power explicitly retires unproved lineage; existing power transfer behavior is unchanged.

Old native effect frames with missing history and malformed root/source/application/current-occurrence data refuse through the single R495 `NotYet` guard. They are not reconstructed by scanning effects. Direct synthetic harness frames without native provenance remain unowned unless the fixture explicitly initializes a root. No general cleanup switch may later treat such absent history as proof that no earlier effects existed.
