# R517 regular-mover pile release and cleanup

2026-11-06. RM23-2-5 releases under cards to their owners' Drop Areas when the top card moves from Battle, Combo or Unison to an area with a different name. RM23-2-6 preserves the pile on a same-named controller transfer. This is a manual-derived correction, not an owner ruling. The old primitive's held-area predicate incorrectly preserved piles across Battle–Combo movement.

The primitive now compares the actual source and destination names after native redirects. Recorded Z-Leader restoration remains separate and precedes ordinary release. Under-placement's distinct recursive-release branch is not changed or claimed covered.

The new private synchronous helper captures every original material occurrence and its R495 metadata before a regular primitive. It retains the genuine `underOrRemoved` ownership occurrence and separately records the effective host area for cleanup. Tokens are specific to one board and cannot be saved or transferred to a clone. Every projection captures its own token. The helper validates all actual returned release records and final physical identities before changing effects, consumes only genuine releases, ends their target effects and applicable while-in-Battle source effects, and settles effect-ownership metadata. Retained or restored attachments are validated but not cleaned. It emits existing effect-ended bookkeeping only; callers own movement logs, triggers and standing settlement. The helper is not an independently authenticated native movement oracle.

The script host now preflights the actual primitive, top and released-material cleanup, R495 settlement and final standing settlement on a clone before live writes. It repeats the captured operation on live state and logs material moves before the top movement without generating material movement Autos. Original top movement history remains in its established path. A no-pile move avoids capture allocation. Existing affected standing stack/Hidden/control restrictions remain.

Flow movement now uses the same pre-capture, cloned preflight and cleanup, revalidating capture after its historical pre-mutation callback. The actual callback audit finds only KO history capture/logging before mutation; current beforeTriggers callers stage from Hand, Energy or under and clear/reset source effects, so their valid sources have no release pile. Live callbacks are never invoked on projections, and no new blanket callback prohibition is introduced. Ordinary Combo-subject and optional Energy projections capture on their own clone and consume actual released records. Existing standing projections and Combo batch reject stacks; setup creates empty piles; currently admitted keyword outlay projections move Hand/Drop sources, so none is a newly releasing producer. Those bounds remain unchanged. `opts.under` recursive releases remain a separate producer outside this regular-mover contract. No Abducted descriptor or other card admission is added.
