# Own-Drop Counter permissions, R438

BT7-024, BT7-048, BT7-073 and BT7-098 now activate their Counter from Drop through their own typed Permanent. The permission requires paying the card's energy cost and removing the original Extra plus one matching-color hand card. It does not waive energy or grant an ordinary Activate from Drop. Printed Leader conditions, current permission negation, Counter restrictions, complete payment and activation taxes remain required.

The source basis is RM 12-2-3-2, 22-10-5/7 and 5-8-2-2. Each card's own FAQ explicitly confirms that this Drop activation triggers BT10-092's opposing Extra activation Auto. The official images were inspected individually: all four have energy cost one and one specified orb of their own color. Native fixtures set those costs explicitly; the frozen catalog has null specified costs. No catalog or database write is made.

A narrow `permit(counterFromDrop)` carries a versioned SkillPrice for the two removals. Only the exact supported own-self Permanent grants admission; neither general static collection nor generic Drop Extra activation is widened. Native candidates, legal actions, ability availability and rejection explanations share the area/permission and saved-occurrence checks. Alternative-energy offers are unavailable on this route. Energy payment, the strict action-price continuation, R449 marker receipts and existing Counter response timing are composed rather than duplicated.

The strict continuation records the original Drop occurrence and the successful original Drop-to-Removed movement result. A replaced movement cannot count as payment merely because it ends in the same area. The source remains Removed after activation; its event retains `from:drop`, so its own activation Auto and broad Extra watchers fire while explicitly hand-qualified watchers do not. Payment and tax continuations retain their source occurrence, and completed prior payments stay spent on failure. The existing declared Counter tax is mandatory, not a new decline offer. A tax that becomes unaffordable fails the activation; a departed paid source cannot cause a fresh hand occurrence to be moved or activated.

Counter prompts and energy-selection prompts retain optional versioned Drop-candidate epochs. Missing/malformed bindings and leave/reentry cannot silently authorize a fresh occurrence. Payment frames without valid origin/permission metadata require rebuilding. A hand candidate moved into Drop after the response window does not acquire Drop authority from its old candidate ID. Ordinary hand and unrelated saved continuations keep their existing path.

The frozen source and full comparison are in `/workspace/shared/arena-support5/triggers-r438/`: `intent.json`, four official PNGs, `after-full.json` and `full-diff.json`. Against `recovery/thirtysecond-integrated-full.json`, exactly four full records change: BT7-024#10, BT7-048#10, BT7-073#10 and BT7-098#10. Only ops, unread, pattern and reads change. Every other field, including Counter cost/condition, activation Auto, validity and trigger data, is identical. The new permit preserves each printed color and both mandatory removals. All four records were reviewed individually. Corpus totals: 13,964 records; 12,987 read and 977 unread.

`drop-counter-5.ts` passes 164 contract/native cases across both seats and all four stored cards. It covers actual Gotenks back-face draw and the same-name lock on a second attack, energy and payer/Leader negatives, permission negation, no alternate waiver, Counter:Counter decline/negation, tax success/failure, JSON reload, private hand projection, candidate/menu/ability/rejection consistency, stale energy selection and source payment occurrences, and missing/malformed saved bindings. Source/payer departure and corrupted save controls are explicitly staged faults, not invented interleaving during atomic payment. The four helpers also reject other areas and the other player. Existing Counter price/chain, alternative lifecycle, Extra activation, affected-card effect/prohibition audits, language/lang/Teach, rulings, capability inventory, TypeScript and changed-file lint are required compatibility checks.

The new enum crosses the Teach reference's existing whole-tier budget threshold. Its test now compares an explicitly generous reference with a tight reference, retaining default-budget and all-row coverage; no runtime prompt budget was raised. The old effect-audit X-marker assertion is corrected separately by the parent's R449 follow-up and is not part of this implementation commit.
