# Explicit Counter self-play occurrences, R465

An already-activated Counter could self-play a fresh occurrence of its source after it left Drop and returned during a saved response. R432 guarded only retained Counters and compared every selected physical source ID, which could also reject a legitimate ordinary choice of a fresh Drop target. R465 binds explicit self references, replaces that ID-only filter, and leaves independent effect instructions running (RM 3-1-4, 9-3-1, 22-10-7).

Fresh affected Counter effect frames carry versioned variable provenance alongside their captured source occurrence. A self chooser binds its output to that occurrence; ordinary choose/look/reveal/mill/move-as and other output writers invalidate prior self provenance, even when the new value happens to be the same physical ID. Direct self references use the same occurrence check. Payment frames are excluded. The source-program classifier excludes delayed and granted bodies and finds affected old saves even after their chooser has been spliced away. Missing or malformed affected witnesses require rebuilding; unrelated Counter programs remain usable.

A play-tax offer is checked again before payment and play. Its remaining child also carries the source occurrence and parent scope/skill identity. If the original self is gone, only that remaining child is canceled: completed payments remain spent and the Counter parent resumes, skips self-play, and resolves independent instructions. Missing child or parent bindings require rebuilding before another payer moves. Stale tax answers and receipts are cleared so they cannot authorize a later independent play. This is distinct from R453's failure of an unactivated Extra Counter: R465 does not cancel an already-activated Counter effect.

The regression uses actual P-337 from hand, with its existing Battle-only erratum and blue/yellow specified cost from R464. Both ordinary declared play and a skill's future-card window are covered. P-337's incoming-card hand replacement still applies when stale self-play is skipped, and its arrival Auto does not draw without an actual arrival. Ordinary Drop choices can select and play the fresh same-ID source, including paying an ordinary play tax. Source leave/reentry controls deliberately modify saved state at genuine native prompts; they are staged fault controls, not claims that a player may insert an action during atomic resolution. Tax programs are explicit typed mechanism fixtures, not additional printed-card support claims.

`counter-self-binding-contract-5.ts` has 31 helper/interpreter cases. `counter-self-binding-native-5.ts` has 36 native cases, including both seats, JSON reload, ordinary and future play, unchanged sources, stale self-play, ordinary fresh targets, unanswered tax offers, tax children before and after partial payment, and missing/malformed child/parent bindings including a truncated saved parent tail. Compatibility checks cover retained Counters, conditional replacements, choice tails, ordinary Counter payment/response chains and R453's new Extra payment phases.

The frozen census in `/workspace/shared/arena-support5/triggers-r463/self-play-census.json` contains 303 already-readable Counter records: 298 Battle and five Unison records, each with one syntactic immediate self-play. The classifier recognizes all 303. This is shape coverage, not 303 native gameplay proofs. The separate price census in `triggers-r465/self-play-price-census.json` retains the 16 records with action-price programs; none explicitly selects its own source in that price. No compiler, stored rule or language operation changed, so no rule-record refresh or unread reduction is claimed.

R465 does not repair the separate native movement-effect lifetime issue, extend source protection to every self movement, or implement the R463 energy and R466 under-card permissions. Those remain the next work. No DB, paid call, dependency installation, build, full gate or contract emit was used.
