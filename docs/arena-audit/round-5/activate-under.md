## Ordered continuation and saved boundaries

The dedicated continuation captures complete original permission/body/price, source and immediate host identities, physical owners, controller, occurrence and face/Hidden history. The source must have exactly one immediate host and no independent zone placement. It pays ordinary Energy through the existing planner and stores payer IDs, epochs and colors with the canonical expected card price. Green/White payer colors are accepted; the test does not invent a White specified symbol. The catalog's specified-cost fields are null, so the fixture retains the engine's existing unspecified-cost convention rather than claiming an independently verified exact printed symbol count.

Actual staging uses the native movement callback, with completed original movement to the physical owner's Drop and a fresh epoch. The existing callback reports `from: null` for an under card because the board finder has no independent zone there; the separate authentic under-origin capture supplies the original area. No generic writer is changed. A saved continuation must retain that staging occurrence and face, but its historical host may leave or cease to exist after staging.

The exact represented Paragus price retains the printed in-play selector. Both required operations must complete: actual skill negation and the original move to Drop. Protected negation, prohibited movement, automatic redirection and even a replacement to the same Drop cannot pay the price. An optional replacement can pause after negation. Declining completes the original payment; accepting leaves the earlier negation and Extra staging paid but announces no activation or Limit use. The saved continuation binds the chosen payer and its captured matching attributes. Missing/null/malformed receipts, illegal cursors/variables, huge specified counts, stale source staging and malformed payer attributes deliberately refuse without incidental TypeError or RangeError.

Successful completion uses the ordinary activation resolver with its already-paid price, then announces exactly one activation with captured `from: under`, original master and Extra/card-activation flags. The card is never temporarily placed in Hand. Limit is shared with the original skill's Hand copies and other under copies. Matching additional taxes, unimplemented Hand-equivalent discounts/coupons, copied permissions/bodies, nonordinary resource substitutes and staging replacements remain explicit prepayment unsupported boundaries. Ordinary matching card-price layers already applied by the native planner are not discarded.
