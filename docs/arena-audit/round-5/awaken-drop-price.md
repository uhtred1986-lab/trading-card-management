# BT16-020 Awaken Drop payment (R470)

The official 2023-01-01 erratum changes the original life-two OR payment wording to life-four AND payment. Existing catalog errata already preserves that correction. The missing compiler reading is the destination-before-source phrase: place five Saiyan cards under this card from Drop. A bounded Awaken normalization reads the same exact payment without altering the stored printed text or treating the action as an alternative life condition.

Native tests found two existing prerequisite bugs. Fixed-host under-card affordability counted matching source cards without checking whether each could be placed under the host. It now filters each payer through the host's placement prohibition before requiring the complete count. The final host/payer feasibility check also observes the prohibition. Variable chosen-host matching and state-dependent interactions among multiple subsequent placements remain separate planner work.

An Awaken multi-card choice previously captured its selected occurrences only when the last choice completed. A first selected Drop payer could leave or leave and return during later picks, then pay as a fresh card. Each accepted incremental choice now captures its occurrence; completing the choice preserves those stamps. Successful movement updates the stamp for already-paid cards as before. A saved partial choice missing an original stamp requires rebuilding. Invalid or incomplete payment releases neither draw nor flip.

The focused suite loads the actual corrected front and back through stored rules, with both-seat native setup and JSON reloads. Its 33 cases cover life two/four/five, four/five/six payers, wrong trait/area/owner, mandatory exact payment, surplus cards, selected payer departure/reentry, Leader reentry, changed placement prohibition and missing saved payer stamps. Typed prohibition fixtures distinguish four eligible cards plus an ineligible matching fifth from five eligible cards; they do not claim a printed granting card. The existing 110-case Surge suite also passes after the selection fix.
