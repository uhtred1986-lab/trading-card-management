# World Tournament Announcer portrait art

`public/arena/tutorial/announcer-{welcome,explain,thinking,celebrate}.svg` are original SVG fan illustrations created for the Arena tutorial. They depict the Dragon Ball World Tournament Announcer with swept blond hair, dark sunglasses, a dark suit, red tie and a handheld microphone. The four files share a consistent 240 × 300 viewBox and transparent background. The illustration keeps the head and upper torso within the central portrait crop; the arms and microphone stay inside the same bounds. Use `small` (44 CSS px) for a coach portrait and `large` (240 CSS px) for a teaching pose. Vector paths keep edges clear at 32–48 px and at fullscreen display sizes.

The character design was informed by the official character reference at [Dragon Ball Official: Tenkaichi Budokai Announcer](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_833.html), including its linked image [05_08851609_028.png](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/dragonball/jp/news/2022/01/05_08851609_028.png). These are references only. The bundled drawings are original fan art, are not official artwork, and no official artwork license or endorsement is claimed. Nothing loads from those URLs at runtime.

The old tutorial prototype's generic “Max” character remains only in that separate prototype; this component and its local SVGs use the World Tournament Announcer identity. Keep that prototype art available as a placeholder until a production asset is selected. The illustration is not instructional content: tutorial copy and controls must explain each lesson in text.
