# World Tournament Announcer: first match curriculum

**Curriculum ID:** `masters-first-match`
**Version:** `1.0.0`
**Status:** reviewed teaching copy for implementation
**Audience:** a first-time player, with an adult or friend nearby if wanted
**Game:** Dragon Ball Super Card Game Masters only

This lesson uses a short, scripted match with synthetic training cards and three Life cards. It teaches the shape of a turn and one battle. Regular Masters games begin with eight cards in Life under the standard setup rules. This is an introduction, not a complete rules course. Synthetic training cards have no official Q&A entries. Before using a real catalog card in this lesson, review its printed text and the official Q&A for that card.

## Delivery contract

Each lesson has a stable ID. Implementations may change layout, but should preserve IDs unless a deliberate curriculum version change is made. Announcer copy is quoted exactly as shown. Optional explanation is behind a “Why?” control and never blocks play. The five opening screens are full-screen, skippable, and replayable; gameplay lessons stay available in a lesson list. There is no reading timer. A wrong prediction changes no game state and spends no gameplay action. “Skip this question” also leaves game state unchanged.

Lesson progress comes from the active Arena session and rules engine: phase/turn changes, accepted or declined charge, card inspection and play/payment resolution, attack declaration and target, combo commitment, battle resolution, and Life movement. A highlighted control is a pointer to the real Arena interface; it is not a replacement control. Do not infer completion from a click alone, a timer, or DOM inspection.

## Scenario walkthrough for implementation

1. Start at the five opening screens. Skip any screen; replay all five from Lessons. Neither choice changes the session.
2. On `t1.charge`, choose a card and confirm. In a second walkthrough, decline charge and verify progression. A cancelled/declined choice is not reported as a charge.
3. Answer `t1.affordability` incorrectly once. Verify explanatory feedback, retry and skip work without consuming a gameplay action or changing energy. Use the alternate legal one-cost card on the retry path; the lesson follows the card actually selected and its actual payment.
4. Complete the first turn through normal play/payment controls. Confirm the first-player exception message is accurate and no attack or first-turn draw is available.
5. Observe the opponent’s real scripted events, answer the battle prediction incorrectly once, then retry or skip. Commit a legal defensive combo in one path; in another, decline and end Defense. When the final totals tie, verify the attacker wins; also cover a defensive combo that makes the defender stronger and a successful attack that deals Life damage only when the Leader is the guard. Verify combo card goes to Drop or the engine’s actual Z-Energy choice is presented and resolved.
6. On turn two, verify refresh/draw evidence, optional charge, legal attack-target list, the existing confirmation where that input mode presents one, and the opportunity to choose an offensive combo or none. Complete the actual response window and battle. Life moves only if a successful attack reaches the Leader.
7. After the board changes, capture the learner’s decision before showing a hint. Guidance controls remain available throughout; complete with hints either on or off and replay a lesson.

## Rules basis

Rule references below are to `docs/rules/rulemanual.txt` in the checked-in Masters manual. Confirm the cited section against the current manual when editing this curriculum.

| Curriculum claim | Rules reference |
|---|---|
| Standard setup puts eight cards from the top of each deck face-down in Life. The tutorial’s three-Life setup is a scenario shortcut, not the standard rule. | §6-2-1-10 |
| Rested cards refresh; first player does not draw on their first turn; turn player may charge one hand card. | §§7-2-7, 7-2-9/7-2-9-1, 7-2-11 |
| First player cannot attack on their first turn. | §7-3-4-4-1 |
| Active attacker may attack Leader/Unison or a rested Battle Card, subject to text/effects and engine legality. | §8-1-1 |
| Offense and Defense are separate response steps; each player can combo in their own step and end that step. Pass declines the current prompt or ends a response window when exposed by the engine; it is not a general end-turn action. | §§8-2, 8-3 |
| Combo cards add combo power; battle compares totals and attacker wins ties; successful attack on a Leader deals damage. | §§5-7, 8-4, 5-10 |
| Battle End may offer up to one combo Battle Card for Z-Energy; remaining combo cards go to Drop. | §8-5-2 through §8-5-8 |

Card cost, power and combo power are distinct printed values: §§2-6, 2-7 and 2-8. A player loses if their Life or deck runs out, an effect makes them lose, or they concede: §0-1-3. This first lesson does not teach all rules, card-specific skills, or Awakening. Check the selected Leader’s printed text and official Q&A before teaching any real-card exception.
