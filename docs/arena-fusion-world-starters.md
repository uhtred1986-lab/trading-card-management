# Fusion World starter decks FS01–FS04 — printed card text (reference for Arena F1)

Reference data for the `Arena F1 — Fusion World` milestone (`docs/arena-fusion-world-plan.md`). Transcribed from the English card pages of fw.dbscards.fr on 26 Oct 2026; the authority is the printed card and the catalog row in `cards` (`game = "fusion"`). Specified costs are not listed by that site and are an owner data task (F1-08).

Source: fw.dbscards.fr card pages (English text), fetched 2026-10-26. All 64 cards fetched; none missing.
Format: `FSxx-yy | Name | type | colour | cost(spec) | power | combo | text`.
Notes:
- The site does not list a specified cost for any card, so `(spec)` is `(?)` throughout. Combo power is `—` where the site lists none (the 5-cost SRs and the 8-cost Broly : BR).
- The site renders keywords as plain labels run together ("PermanentYour Turn", "Activate MainOnce per turn", "Auto[Opponent's Turn]"); they are normalised here to the bracketed form `[Permanent][Your Turn]`, `[Activate:Main][Once Per Turn]`, `[Auto][Opponent's Turn]`. Fusion World prints "On Play" (not "When Played"); kept as `[On Play]`.
- Keyword reminder texts as printed: [Blocker] "(After an opponent's attack, you may switch this card to Rest Mode and change the target of the attack to this card.)"; [Critical] "(When this card inflicts damage with an attack, the life is placed in the Drop.)"; [Double Strike] "(This card inflicts 2 damage instead of 1 when attacking.)"; [Super Combo] "(A maximum of 4 cards with Super Combo can be placed in a deck.)". Omitted from the lines below for brevity.
- 16 cards have no skill text (vanilla); marked `(no skill text)`.

### Distinct effect sentence patterns (with occurrences)

Draw / hand
- "Draw 1 card." — 10: FS01-01 (×2 sides), 03; FS02-01, 08, 15 ("…draw 1 card" after condition); FS03-01 (front, and back after cost), 10; FS04-01, 02, 07
- "Add up to 1 card from your life to your hand." — 7: FS01-04, 06; FS02-06, 09; FS03-07; FS04-05, 12
- "add up to 1 <Name> … from your Drop to your hand" — 1: FS02-03 (two names, "both with a cost of 2 or less")
- "add up to 1 Extra from your Drop to your hand" — 1: FS02-11
- "Look at 5 cards from the top of your deck, reveal up to 1 <Paragus> or up to 1 Extra and add it to your hand. Place the rest at the bottom of your deck in a random order." — 1: FS03-13
- "If you have 7 or fewer cards in your hand, …" (Blue hand-size condition) — 7: FS02-01, 03, 06, 07, 09, 11, 15, 16

Power modification
- "this card gets +N power for the turn" — FS02-01 (+15000), FS03-01 (+5000)
- "Choose up to 1 of your Battle Cards and it gets +5000 power for the battle" — FS01-01 back
- "Choose up to 1 of your other Battle Cards and it gets +5000 for the turn" — FS01-12
- "Choose your Leader or up to 1 of your Battle Cards and it gets +15000 power for the battle." — 4: FS01-15, FS02-15, FS03-15, FS04-16 (the four 1-cost combat-trick Extras)
- "that card gets +10000 power for the battle" (conditional second step) — FS03-15
- "[Permanent] This card gets +N power" (unconditional in a timing window) — FS01-04 (+5000 Your Turn), FS01-06 (+5000 Opponent's Turn)
- "[Permanent] If you have 7 or fewer cards in your hand, this card gets +N power" — FS02-07 (+15000), FS02-09 (+10000)
- "[Permanent] If you have 6 or more energy, this card gets +5000 power" — FS03-11
- "Choose up to 1 of your opponent's Battle Cards and it gets -N power for the turn" — 4: FS01-02 (-5000), 08 (-20000), 09 (-10000), 15 (-5000)
- "this card gets +10000 combo power" (Super Combo) — 4
- No "+N power for each …" pattern in the starters.

Removal
- "Choose up to 1 of your opponent's Battle Cards with N power or less and KO it" — FS01-16 (30000)
- "Choose up to 1 of your opponent's Battle Cards and KO it" (unconditional) — FS03-10
- "Choose up to 1 of your opponent's Rest Mode Battle Cards with a cost of N or less and KO it" — FS04-04 (4), FS04-15 (3)
- "Choose up to 1 of your opponent's Battle Cards with a cost of N or less and place it at the bottom of its owner's deck" — FS02-13 (4), FS02-16 (5)
- "choose up to 1 of your opponent's Battle Cards with a cost of 2 or less and return it to its owner's hand" — FS02-06

Rest / Active mode
