BT31-093 front skill 20 and BT31-150 front skill 30 trigger when their own card is played. Their later opponent-play clause describes the act taxed under 20-14-1, not another Auto activation. This is the existing reading documented and tested in `scripts/verify/taxes.ts`; no new owner ruling is inferred.

The trigger reader and effect compiler now share the same play-tax wording normalization. The registered tax suite pins both full catalog records to exactly `played`, preserves genuine single/alternative opponent-play Autos, and plays the printed Auto followed by the opponent's taxed play across JSON reloads. The tax is paid to Warp while the original draw and removal choice do not recur.

`evidence/812/printed-skills.json` freezes the two public catalog skill texts. The compiler comparison uses one frozen live catalog snapshot before and after the code change; no stored rules are written. Corrected records remain protected.

The frozen-catalog comparison covers 6,552 cards and 13,615 skills. Exactly two records change, both only in `trigger`: `[played, opponentPlayed]` becomes `[played]`. Operations, conditions, costs, unsupported clauses and readings are identical. The complete delta is `evidence/812/compiler-diff.json`.
