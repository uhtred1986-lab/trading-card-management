Refs #907, #940. "[Auto] When this card is **played or attacks**, ..." (BT25-128, P-575, BT10-045, BT30-009, BT17-090, EX24-06 and about twenty more) was recorded with the trigger `played` alone and, when the rest of the sentence read, nothing unread, so the attack half never fired. "When this card in your hand is **played or placed in your energy**" (P-353) and about forty other coordinated "played or ..." pairs were reduced to `played` the same way.

Official text (dbs-cardgame.com card list): BT25-128 "When this card is played or attacks, ..."; P-353 "When this card in your hand is played or placed in your energy, ..."; BT26-100 "When this card is played or attacks **an opponent's Battle Card**, ..." (the catalog feed prints "attacks and opponent's Battle Card").

What changed:
- `text/triggers.ts`: `attacks` also answers "when this card is played [from your hand] or attacks", "when you play this card [from your hand] or when it attacks" (`PLAYED_OR_ATTACKS`; only when the sentence stops at "attacks"). `PLAYED_FROM_HAND` accepts the "or attacks" tail, so "played from your hand or attacks" is `playedFromHand` + `attacks`.
- `playedOrUnplacedAlternative` (was `playedOrUnplacedLeave`, #940; `compile/index.ts`): any "played or <X>" whose X no reader places, or a play qualified by something no reader keeps ("using [Union-Potara]"), is refused as an unread clause. Placed by their own readers and not refused: "or attacks", "or used in a combo", "or switched to Revealed/Hidden Mode", the removal/KO halves of #940, "using [Over Realm] or [Dark Over Realm]", "from your hand or Drop Area".
- Proof: `scripts/verify/played-or-attacks.ts` (stored-row JSON round trip, real VM, both seats): play pends once, attack pends once, [Once per turn] spans both, neighbouring play-only / attack-only skills unchanged, refused alternatives do nothing.

Known limits, left as they were:
- "Played or placed in your energy" (P-353, EX19 Bardock: Xeno family) has no trigger for a hand card placed in the energy area; it is now an honest gap.
- "Attacks an opponent's Battle Card" (BT26-100) needs a target condition on the attack half only.
- BT9-046/-059/-061/-120/-121/-122 ("you play this card or it is placed in a Drop Area from a Battle Area by a skill") and the "discarded by a skill" Shadow Dragon family (BT14/BT15/P-313) could get their second half taught; they are gaps now.

Acceptance:
- [x] "Played [from your hand] or attacks" keeps both halves in the stored WHEN.
- [x] Stored rows -> real VM: play once, attack once, once per turn across both, reload, both seats.
- [x] An alternative nothing can place stays an unread gap.
- [x] "Attacks an opponent's Battle Card" stays a gap.
- [x] Glossary, typecheck, lint, tests, build. No database writes. Full-catalog reading diff by `arena:readings` (public feed); a database `arena:diff` is the owner's.
