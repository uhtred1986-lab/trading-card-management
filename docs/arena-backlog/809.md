The obsolete blanket refusal in `runtimeSupportOfRow` is removed. Native counter-motion chains already implement the response window (#552, manual 9-7-3 and 22-10); affordability remains a live-board decision. Unread clauses, Auto prices, missing triggers, permanent restrictions and board-dependent play targets retain their existing checks.

The registered `counter-chain-552` suite now loads its Counter: Counter skills from hand-authored confirmed records via `rulesFromRows`. All existing payment, decline, unaffordable, alternating-chain, negation, reload and privacy scenarios therefore exercise the previously broken stored-record boundary. Additional assertions preserve independent unread and board-dependent reasons.

The read-only live comparison in `evidence/809/readiness.json` covers 21 Counter: Counter records: 2 have no remaining static blocker, 10 still have unsupported clauses, and 9 have only board-dependent play uncertainty. No database rows were written. This is skill-level readiness, not a claim that every containing deck is ready.
