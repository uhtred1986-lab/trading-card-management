Priority P1. Found by the 20 October 2026 rules review, batch 3 (receipt: https://github.com/uhtred1986-lab/trading-card-management/blob/main/docs/arena-backlog/evidence/rules-review-2026-10-20/batch-3.md, section 3, row BT29-065 front 40).

## Problem

BT29-065 Vegeta, Back From the Brink of Death, front 40 is an [Auto] that is meant to fire from the Drop: when your life moves to another area, it is played from your Drop (its Leader condition is {Vegeta, Warrior Race Pride}). The engine never offers it.

Code path: in the `damage` case of the script interpreter (`src/lib/arena/vm/script.ts`, `case "damage"`, around line 1856), the life-left trigger is pended only for cards in play: `for (const w of h.cardsInPlay(p)) h.pend("lifeLeft", w, taken[0]);` (around line 1884). A card in the Drop Area is never given the pend, so the trigger collection never sees it.

Engine proof (20 Oct 2026): Leader {Vegeta, Warrior Race Pride}, BT29-065 in the Drop, one point of damage to life: no offer is made.

The other three faces of the card are confirmed (front 10, 20, 30: +10000 power at life 6, 4, 2), so only front 40 is in scope.

## Source of truth

- `docs/rules/rulemanual.txt` 3-9-4: a player may move any card when a card is moved from the Life Area to another area.
- 9-1-3-2: [Activate], [Auto] and [Counter] skills may name the area they act from ("play ... from ..."), which is what makes a Drop-origin Auto legal.
- Official FAQ for BT29-065: not checked in this issue. If the FAQ contradicts the manual, the FAQ wins (`docs/arena-backlog.md` section 5).

## Build

1. `src/lib/arena/vm/script.ts`, the `damage` case: also pend `lifeLeft` to cards in the Drop Area whose [Auto] names the Drop as its origin. Read that origin from the compiled record (the trigger's declared area), not from the card's name or ID. The Battle Area and Leader cases stay as they are.
2. `src/lib/arena/vm/triggers.ts`: the trigger collection must let a Drop-zone card's `lifeLeft` Auto through only when its declared origin is the Drop. A Drop card without such an Auto must not fire (no new fires).
3. `src/lib/arena/glossary.ts`: the `lifeLeft` / Drop-origin entry, in the same commit.

## Out of scope

- Other Drop-origin Autos. Find them with the same declared-origin test, but fix only BT29-065 front 40 here; list the rest as a follow-up.
- The BT29-065 row. Rules are records; the engine must read the row as stored.
- Permanents and the other three faces.
