## Evidence, 18 October 2026 (the four bullets that were open)

`npm run arena:tutorial-browser` (input 12, motion 10, boards 21 and guided 48 checks, all pass). Screenshots and `results.json`: `docs/arena-backlog/evidence/tutorial/`. The match is played through the page's real controls (tap/click on cards, Charge it, Play it, attacker then target, End turn, response buttons); the session is only read to know which prompt is up.

- Input mode (new): `src/components/arena/tutorial/use-input-mode.ts` starts from `pointer: coarse` / `fine` and follows the last real input event (touch or pen pointer, mouse pointer, navigation key). `compactCoach(lesson, snapshot, mode)` words the instruction for that mode and `INPUT_HELP` gives one line per mode, the other two kept under "Other ways to play". Proved on a coarse phone (touch wording), a mouse press and a Tab key on the same phone (mouse, then keyboard wording), a finger tap (back to touch), a 390 px window with a mouse (mouse: width does not decide), and an 820 px touch device (touch). Keyboard flow checked: Tab reaches a hand card and Enter offers its Charge button. The mouse wording does not claim a double-click: `Hand.tsx` disables `onDoubleTap` in browse mode, so the board's own "double-click a hand card to charge it" prompt text does not work today (see the PR).
- Anchors: at every checkpoint (charge, play review, own attack picked / target, combo response, hand-off) the lesson cue outlines at least one visible anchor, and the coach (position: relative, in flow) never overlaps or covers the hand or the dock. Landscape resize with a review open keeps the review and the cue; reload with a review open restores accepted moves and closes the unconfirmed review. Fixed: the `decision` cue outlined only the hand toolbar, which is hidden on phones; it now also outlines the dock (`data-arena-dock`, one attribute on the existing dock wrapper in `ArenaStage.tsx`).
- Preserve board info: with hints off the settled board text is identical to hints on at 390, 820 and 1440; Restore hints brings the coach back; all fourteen zone anchors are present; the coach never sits over the hand or the dock.
- Motion: the only animation is the announcer's 0.35 s arrival on the intro (absent under `prefers-reduced-motion`); the intro-to-compact-coach swap and the focus cue are immediate and static outlines, so reduced motion has nothing to skip and no lesson text disappears. The board's own beats keep playing with the coach up (a Skip control is offered while the opponent moves).
- Scenario coverage: charge, alternate charge (last card, not the suggested one), unaffordable card (no move, no progress), play review, own attack and target, opponent attack with a combo response, resize with a review open. Not exercised: an explicit multi-option payment prompt and the "Confirm attack" step (neither occurred on the phone flow: exact energy pays automatically and a target tap declares the attack), and a desktop-mouse attack.
