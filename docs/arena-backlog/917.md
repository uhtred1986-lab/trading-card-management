## Shared printed permission
BT19-077 SSG Son Goku, Crimson Impact; BT19-078 SS Son Goku, All-Out Evolution; BT19-081 SSG Vegeta, Crimson Impact; BT19-082 SS Vegeta, All-Out Evolution print:

> [Permanent] This card's [EX-Evolve] can also be activated from your Z-Energy.

This is a source-area extension for the EVOLVING card. It is not #913's permission to choose the evolution BASE in energy, and grants no Active Mode override. Reuse the existing EX-Evolve semantics and generic source-area permission machinery where available; do not ask the owner to reinterpret the already-verified keyword.

**Source:** `docs/rules-audit-2026-10-15/audit.json`; `docs/rules/rulemanual.txt` 0-2-2 and 22-5; `src/lib/arena/rulesets/dbs/keywords.rules`; `src/lib/arena/vm/activate.ts`; `src/lib/arena/vm/actions.ts`; `src/lib/arena/vm/play.ts`; `src/lib/arena/compile/effects.ts`; `src/lib/arena/glossary.ts`; `docs/agent-brief.md`.

## Verified complete costs/conditions
All four EX-Evolve lines print [Limit 1], one green energy AND draw 1 card as the skill cost. BT19-077/081 require green Son Goku: Br / Vegeta: Br respectively costing <=3; BT19-078/082 require their respective green character costing <=2. Respect character identity, not just a matching name substring. Other Auto/keyword skills on these cards are outside scope except normal regression behavior.

## Delivered (PR for #917)

Official basis: the card Q&A on BT19-077 (Q1: "you can activate [EX-Evolve] on this card while it is in your Z-Energy and then play it on top of the target card in the Battle Area"); manual 22-5-3, 22-5-4 (the hand-only default the printed Permanent overrides), 22-5-5, 22-5-5-1, 22-5-7; game Q&A on [Evolve]/[EX-Evolve] (Main Phase only, play/evolved Autos fire, rested base, old granted skills do not transfer) is the ordinary behaviour kept. The errata page lists none of BT19-077/078/081/082; the card Q&A has an entry for BT19-077 only (checked 18 Oct 2026).

The Permanent compiles to one self-scoped `permit` of `evolveFromZEnergy`. `actions.rules`' `activate` also considers the Z-Energy Area, `activationsOf` keeps only an Evolve keyword line there and only with the permit, and `keywords.rules`' Evolve refuses unless the card is in hand, or in Z-Energy holding that permit. `keywordActivated` already carries the real source zone, so `evolveFromHandActivated` does not fire for a Z-Energy use. A failed Z-Energy evolution puts the card in its owner's Drop (22-5-7 names no area). Proof: `scripts/verify/z-evolve-917.ts`.

Judgement calls: (1) the exact printed order of `[Limit 1]` and the cost is not in the public sources checked, so the fixtures print `[EX-Evolve][Limit 1]{g}, draw 1 card:` as the issue describes it; (2) the Z-Energy evolve is a labelled best reading of 22-5-7 for failure: Drop, as from hand.
