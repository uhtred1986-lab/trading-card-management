## Problem and scope
Seven cards share the same unresolved Permanent extending their [EX-Evolve] base choice to energy and specifying Active Mode:

> [Permanent] When using this card's [Evolve] skill from your hand, you can choose Battle Cards from your energy and play this card in Active Mode.

DB3-027 Bardock, Great Ape Assault; DB3-030 King Vegeta, Great Ape Assault; DB3-033 Tora, Great Ape Assault; DB3-035 Fasha, Great Ape Assault; DB3-037 Shugesh, Great Ape Assault; DB3-039 Borgos, Great Ape Assault; DB3-042 Nappa, Promising Youth.

Example DB3-027 prints [EX-Evolve] {u}{u}: Blue <Bardock> with an energy cost of 2 or more. This is a shared compiler and rules-VM capability increment, not seven hand-authored exceptions. The current Evolve DO in keywords.rules chooses its base IN you.battle. Reuse generic alternative-area/keyword machinery where possible; do not add a parallel Evolve engine or hardcoded card IDs. Do not fix unrelated Autos on the seven cards by approximation.

**Source:** `docs/rules/rulemanual.txt` section 22-5; `src/lib/arena/rulesets/dbs/keywords.rules`; `src/lib/arena/vm/activate.ts`; `src/lib/arena/vm/actions.ts`; `src/lib/arena/vm/play.ts`; `src/lib/arena/compile/effects.ts`; `src/lib/arena/glossary.ts`; `docs/agent-brief.md`.

22-5-1/3/4: activate from hand during Main Phase, pay the printed skill cost, choose a qualifying specified-character Battle Card in your Battle Area by default. 22-5-2-2 explicitly includes EX-Evolve/Xeno-Evolve when text refers to Evolve skills. 22-5-3-1 allows printed skills to expand the base-selection area. 22-5-3-1-1: when resolving an expanded-area evolution that would play the evolving card, first move the chosen base to the Battle Area, then resolve the keyword normally; area-movement triggers from that preliminary move do not become pending and it opens no counter timing. This does not silently remove the normal new-card play/counter handling.

22-5-5: stack the evolving card on the base; retain position/power changes under ordinary Evolve, with explicit card-text overrides. The common Permanent supplies Active Mode on the expanded energy-base path. Verify the exact scope of the mode override against full printed text/manual/FAQ before implementing broader behavior; preserve ordinary Evolve/Xeno behavior outside the supported scope and report a genuinely unresolved ruling rather than guessing.

Game Q&A confirms ordinary EX-Evolve over a rested base enters rested, new-card play/evolution Autos can activate, and old granted skills do not transfer. Card Q&A specifically says evolving DB3-027 over Rest Mode TB3-019 Final Strike Bardock in energy does NOT activate the underlying Bardock's Auto. The same negative is stated for DB3-030/033/035/037/039 with their named energy bases. Final Strike Bardock's Auto is a Rest-to-Active effect; testing only generic area-movement suppression would miss this requirement. No DB3-042-specific FAQ entry was found.
