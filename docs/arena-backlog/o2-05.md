## Delivered

`src/lib/arena/policy/choices.ts` (pure, bounded, no RNG): compound card picks sized and ranked by whose cards they
are and what the resolving skill's sentence does to them (the opponent's / the seat's gain: as many as allowed, most
valuable first; a cost: the fewest it must, least valuable first; an agreed price paid in full; "different names" and
a 22-30-3 colour cover honoured), scored options for modes, replacements (9-10), Auto prices, [Offering] and "you
may", Auto order by trigger order, and `WINDOW_HANDLERS`, the coverage matrix (every `DECISION_WINDOWS` kind to a
layer; a missing kind fails to compile). `localPolicy()` is shortcuts → Main Phase → battle → choices →
`interruptingPolicy`: an unreadable question throws `PolicyInterruption`, which `guardedDecide` and `localStep`
return as `interrupted` (a referee question too); #647's stack is `combatPolicy`. `types.ts`, `vm/index.ts` and
`vm/costs.ts` were read, not changed: no engine change was needed. `optionalCost` and `offering` are legacy-engine
prompt kinds; the rules engine asks both as `chooseMode`, so they are proved on contract-built inputs with the legacy
engine's own answers, and their rules-engine form is staged. Local [Empower] carries read the cap off the offered
amounts (the board's prompt view has no `max`; the online adapter is unchanged).

Proof: `scripts/verify/arena-policy-choices.mts` in `npm test`. Strength: `npm run arena:policy-eval -- --audit
--baselines combat647,control --games 20` gave 103-77 against the #647 policy over 180 games (57 %, 95 % CI 50-64 %;
synthetic fixtures 57-43, audit decks 46-34) beside 96-84 for the control: a small edge, not a decisive one.

Found, not fixed (engine, out of scope): after taking a "discard instead" substitute (9-10), answering its "up to"
discard with no card still keeps the card in play (`Q-PILEOFF` in the fixture); the policy pays such a price in full
rather than exploit it. Auto price options read "Pay: nothing" for hand/life-card prices (`vm/flow.ts`), so the policy
weighs them as an unknown price of about one card.
