Refs #907, #962. #963 turned the coordinated "played or <X>" triggers whose second half nothing could place into honest unread gaps. Two families have a second half the engine already had a moment for:

- The Shadow Dragon family (BT14-131..135, BT14-137, BT15-130..137, P-313): "If your Leader Card is a ≪Shadow Dragon≫ card: When this card is **played from your hand or discarded by a ≪Shadow Dragon≫ card's skill**, you may look at the top card of your deck; if it's a black ≪Shadow Dragon≫ card you may add it to your hand, otherwise, place it at the bottom of your deck." BT14-138 prints the discard half alone.
- BT9-046, -059, -061, -120, -121, -122: "When you play this card **or it is placed in a Drop Area from a Battle Area by a skill**, ...".

What changed:
- `text/triggers.ts`: `droppedFromHand` also answers "played [from your hand] or discarded by <cause> skill" and "when this card is discarded by <cause> skill"; `PLAYED_FROM_HAND` accepts that tail, so the card's WHEN is `playedFromHand` + `droppedFromHand`. `droppedFromBattle` also answers "when you play this card or it is placed in a Drop Area from a Battle Area by a skill" (`PLAYED_OR_DROPPED_FROM_BATTLE`), beside `played`. `playedOrUnplacedAlternative` places both (and still refuses a tail it cannot read: "or KO'd", "placed in a Warp", an unreadable cause).
- `compile/index.ts`: the cause ("by a ≪Shadow Dragon≫ card's skill") is a condition on the trigger's subject, `any[not(subject), subject matches]`, so it qualifies the discard half only (a play has no subject). A description `filterFor` cannot read refuses the Auto.
- `vm/script.ts`: the discard hands the skill's card on as the subject (`pend("droppedFromHand", id, frame.card)`), as `deckToWarpBySkill` already did. Nothing else about when it fires changed: a skill's effect or price taking the card out of its owner's hand into the Drop (20-7-1, 20-7-4); a move the rules make, a KO and a play from the Drop do not.
- `compile/effects.ts`: "if it's a black ≪Shadow Dragon≫ card **you may add it to your hand**" (no comma): the action rode on the look's condition and was swallowed, so the family's "add it to your hand" half was silently lost. It is put back as the clause after the condition.
- Proof: `scripts/verify/played-or-discarded.ts` (stored-row JSON round trip, real VM, both seats).

Official text (Bandai card list, BT14-131): "If your Leader Card is a ≪Shadow Dragon≫ card: When this card is played from your hand or discarded by a ≪Shadow Dragon≫ card's skill, you may look at the top card of your deck; if it's a black ≪Shadow Dragon≫ card you may add it to your hand, otherwise, place it at the bottom of your deck." 20-7-3 asks no area of the discarding skill's card. No card FAQ entry for the family was found in the part of the Q&A page that could be read.

Known limits:
- The cause card is read when the Auto resolves, not when the card was discarded.
- A [Once per turn] cannot be shared by a play and a later removal of the same copy: the card that left the Battle Area is a new card (3-1-4); the proof shows one skill carries both alternatives.
- BT9-046 also prints "you can't activate {Toppo, Gaze of Justice}'s skill for the duration of the turn", which the effect reader does not report as unread (it did the same for the play half before #963); not touched here.
- Still gaps from #962: "played or placed in your energy" (P-353 ...), "attacks an opponent's Battle Card" (BT26-100), the other "played or ..." tails.
