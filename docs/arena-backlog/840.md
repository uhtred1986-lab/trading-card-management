## Outcome
A child can explain their next decision, not merely copy a highlighted click. Produce the reviewed teaching script before implementation; use short concrete sentences, one concept per screen and no reading timer. This is a first-match Masters lesson, not a claim to teach all Masters mechanics.

## Scope and acceptance
- [ ] Write exact announcer text, concept, real UI anchor, completion evidence, recovery copy, and optional deeper explanation for each step. Establish stable lesson IDs and a versioned curriculum.
- [ ] Full-screen opening: leader/goal; hand versus life/deck; card cost versus power versus combo power; ready/rested energy. Keep opening to 4–5 skippable, replayable screens, then teach by doing.
- [ ] Player turn 1: optional one-card charge, choose energy thoughtfully, inspect/play a one-cost card, read its actual payment, explain first-player no attack/no draw, end turn. Opponent turn: observe charge/play and one safe attack; learner predicts damage, tries defensive combo and sees that a tie favors the attacker. Player turn 2: observe refresh/draw, charge, choose attacker and legal target, read confirmation, optional offensive combo, resolve damage/life-to-hand.
- [ ] Explain that a played Battle Card can normally attack that turn; do not invent summoning sickness. Leader targets versus rested Battle Card targets must match legal actions and card exceptions. Explain Pass per response window, not as a universal end-turn button. Combo cards leave the hand and are spent; explain the real Z-Energy choice if shown.
- [ ] Include two low-pressure prediction checks (energy affordability; battle power/tie), with explanatory feedback and retry/skip. Wrong answers do not consume a gameplay action. On a changed board, ask for an unguided next decision before offering guidance off/on.
- [ ] Distinguish the short three-life fixture from regular eight-life setup. Describe other win/loss conditions and advanced skills as later lessons; flag awakening as card-specific and avoid implying this simplified leader teaches it.
- [ ] Cite rulemanual.txt §§6-2-1-10, 7-2-7/9/11, 7-3-4-4-1, 8-1 through 8-5, 5-7 and 5-10 for relevant lines. Synthetic training cards have no official Q&A; explicitly say so. Real catalog card adoption requires printed text and official Q&A review first.

**Proposed new file:** docs/arena-tutorial-curriculum.md. Scenario proof: trace every lesson through a paper walkthrough including wrong prediction, declined charge and alternate legal card. Haiku/Luna-sized writing task; a rules-aware reviewer checks wording before merge.
