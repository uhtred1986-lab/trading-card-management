**Source:** #788 spec §11 (https://github.com/uhtred1986-lab/trading-card-management/issues/788#issuecomment-6171895727) · prototype `docs/arena-redesign/prototype-788/` (PR #789), every board in the sky skin. **Tracker:** #795.

## Why
Owner, 14 Oct: the sky skin's brightness and Dragon Ball feel are right, but the boxes make the UI too busy. The skin puts a 2 px ink outline and a hard offset shadow on nearly everything: the top bar, the phase row, both player strips, your row, the lane pill, the dock and every button. Together they read as a grid of boxes.

## Build (`src/app/globals.css`, the `[data-skin="anime"]` rules)
- **Keep:** the sky gradient and clouds (`html[data-skin="anime"] body`), the halftone screentone, the Dragon Ball type, and the card bezels.
- **Keep the ink edge only on objects:** cards, the turn pill and the primary button.
- **Top bar and phase row:** transparent, with no bottom rule, so they sit on the sky.
- **Player strips, lane pill, clash/summary card, dock:** borderless frosted white (about 62–90 % white, with backdrop blur) and a soft shadow instead of the ink outline and offset shadow. The dock gets rounded top corners.
- **Your row:** drop the outlined box (`arena-row-on::before`) and keep a soft white glow.
- **Turn frame (`arena-edge`):** a line at the top and bottom only.
- **Cards:** keep the bezel, and use a soft drop shadow instead of the hard 2×3 px offset.
- **Secondary buttons, tags and chips:** carried by colour or a hairline, with no ink ring.
- The night skin is unchanged.
