**Source:** #788 spec §7 (https://github.com/uhtred1986-lab/trading-card-management/issues/788#issuecomment-6171895727) · prototype scenes `play`, `notarget`, `target`, `bighandTarget`. **Tracker:** #795. **After:** UX 3 (#798, the engine field) and UX 4 (hand and card view).

## Build (`shared-sheets.tsx`, `stage/PromptPanel.tsx`, `stage/QuietNotice.tsx`)
- **Payment:** the play action shows which energy chips it rests (they also blink in your strip) and "4 ⚡ of 6 ready → 2 left". It warns when this leaves nothing for a Counter you hold.
- **No valid target, before commit:** from #798's field, the card view says "You can play this card — but its skill has no target", with the reason. The button reads **Play anyway · 2 ⚡**. The #783 notice stays as the fallback when a target disappears after commit.
- **A targeting card** lists who it can choose and who it can't, and why, before you play it.
- **Choosing a target:**
  - Valid cards glow and say what a tap does ("Tap to KO").
  - The others carry a short reason that never truncates ("✕ Leader", "✕ Barrier"). On a Leader the tag sits at the bottom, clear of the LEADER tag.
  - The prompt lists every "can't be chosen" reason in full, also at 360×650.
  - **Pass · KO nothing** covers an "up to" choice.
