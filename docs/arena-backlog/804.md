**Found by:** the #793 worker. The SD15 starter list, saved as a player's own deck, is refused for offline play: BT9-031 and DB2-004 report "the rules engine cannot resolve an Auto action or X price yet (#149)". Online play refuses to run those skills on confirmed records too. **Tracker:** #577.

## Cause

`runtimeSupportOfRow` in `src/lib/arena/rules-store.ts` adds that reason to every `auto` row whose cost has a program or an X:

```ts
if (kind === "auto" && (cost?.program?.length || cost?.x)) {
  unsupported.push("the rules engine cannot resolve an Auto action or X price yet (#149)");
}
```

Since #549 (and #686) the VM does pay Auto action and resource prices, including X (`src/lib/arena/vm/auto-costs.ts`). `capability-inventory.ts` lists them as proven by `scripts/verify/compiler.ts` (AUTOCOST paid and declined), `scripts/verify/auto-costs-549.ts` and `scripts/verify/last-prints-price-549.ts`. The gate predates that work and was never narrowed. Every card whose [Auto] has a price ("Choose 1 Extra Card in your hand and place it in your Drop Area:", "Choose 1 card in your life and add it to your hand:", …) therefore has its skill skipped online, and is refused offline (#793).

## Build

- Narrow the gate to the prices the engine still cannot pay. Ask the VM's own price planner, statically, or reuse the planner's "not yet" classification (`NotYet` in `auto-costs.ts`, `#549`), instead of refusing every priced Auto.
- Keep the "unreadable price is not reported supported" guard that `scripts/verify-readiness.mts` asserts. A price the planner cannot plan must still be refused with a specific reason.
- Add a regression test on the real texts of BT9-031 and DB2-004:
  - the gate reports them supported;
  - in a game the price is offered, paid or declined, and the effect resolves.
- Glossary rule (CLAUDE.md): if the change alters what the engine claims to play, update `src/lib/arena/glossary.ts`.
- Report which catalog cards change status, using the committed snapshot or the rules fixtures, not the database.
