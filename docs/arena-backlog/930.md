## 25 October: BT28-079 back#0 and SD19-04 front#20 proven (pending record application)

The two standing [Union] reductions are already read by the current compiler —
the 15 Oct "unread" labels were stale — and need no compiler or VM change.
BT28-079's awakened back "[permanent] Reduce the skill cost of [union] skills
on yellow <Vegito> cards in your hand by {1}" and SD19-04's "[permanent] If
your Leader is a Z-Leader or you have a Z-Battle Card in play, reduce the skill
cost of this card's [union] skill in your hand by {g}" are each one standing
`costReduction(what: skill, tag: union)` over hand cards, read where a line's
price is bound (`vm/costs.ts` `skillOrbs`). "[union]" reaches every
[Union-(type)] line (22-13-3-1); "in your hand" leaves out [Union-Absorb],
which is activated from the Battle Area (22-13-6-2); `{1}` takes only a
colourless orb and `{g}` only a green one (5-8-2-4). Proof:
`scripts/verify/union-reductions-930.ts` (printed texts, stored-row JSON and
rules-language round trip, both seats, per-step reload; applies exactly to the
cards and lines named, not to the opponent's, other colours/names, a non-Union
line, an Absorb in play, a coloured-only price, or SD19-04's line without its
[Permanent]; SD19-04's printed "4 or more energy" condition still applies).

Status: **supported in code, pending owner record application** — the live
`card_rules` rows BT28-079/back/0 and SD19-04/front/20 still hold the old
unread records until the owner applies the recompiled ones
(`arena:refresh-ids`).

The five one-use "the next time you activate the [union] skill …" reductions
(BT31-042 f#0, BT31-043 f#20, EX23-11 f#10, EX23-29 f#10, EX25-11 f#10) are a
different mechanism (a resolved one-use `skillCost` effect, `uses: 1`,
`until: turn`, spent by `spendSkillCostUses`) and stay open; per card see the
#923 residual matrix. Open ruling question for all five: the reduction's
targets are the matching cards in the hand **when the effect resolves**, so a
matching card that reaches the hand later that turn is not reduced.
Suggested answer: identify the card at activation ("the next time you
activate … on a … card from your hand"), i.e. a turn-long, one-use reduction
keyed by the description rather than by the cards in hand at resolution.
