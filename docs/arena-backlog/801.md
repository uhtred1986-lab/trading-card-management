## Build (`stage/BattleParts.tsx`, `stage/DuelBand.tsx`, `stage/Takeover.tsx`, `stage/StageZones.tsx`, `stage/PromptPanel.tsx`)
**Declaring an attack:**
- Tap your card, then **⚔ Attack with …**.
- Valid targets are labelled on the card ("Tap to attack", "Rested · tap"). Active Battle Cards show "✕ Active", and the prompt explains the rule: only the Leader or a rested Battle Card can be attacked.

**Attacker → target, before you commit:**
- An arrow from attacker to target, and ATTACKER and TARGET tags on both cards.
- A summary card in the lane names both cards, their role ("Claude's Leader", "Your Battle Card") and their power. The power includes the attacker's own "when it attacks" bonus.
- It gives the outcome ("Tie → attacker wins · Claude takes 1 damage · life 5 → 4"), with Claude's line on top to match the board.
- Every number comes from the snapshot's `battle` and `stakes`.

**Defend, as one stepper: ① Counter ② Block ③ Combo**, in the engine's order (`vm/battle.ts`), one plain question per step:
- "Cancel the attack with a Counter card?": the option is the card itself, with its effect and cost, or why it can't be afforded.
- "Let a [Blocker] take the hit instead?": "4,000 vs 25,000: it is KO'd · you lose no life".
- "Add combo power to Vegeta?": glowing cards, with a live meter ("10,000 + 15,000 = 25,000 vs 25,000 → tie: the attacker wins ties · need 5,000 more").
- A step with nothing to offer is skipped and marked "none".
- During Counter and Block the hand folds to one line ("not needed for this answer"); it returns for Combo.
- The result spells out the rule ("a tie goes to the attacker").

**One consistent Pass:**
- **Pass** is always the last prompt button and always the same shape: the word, and under it what passing means here (don't charge · don't counter · Leader stays target · take the hit · KO nothing · cancel the attack). **End turn** has the same shape ("N can still attack").
- Replays use **⏩ Fast-forward** in the narration line, never "Skip" in the prompt.
- While Claude plays, the prompt says so and shows no button.
