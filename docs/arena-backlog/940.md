Refs #907, #923. BT31-015 (SS Gogeta, Vanquisher) prints “[Auto] When this card is **played or removed from a Battle Area by a skill**, choose up to 1 of your opponent's Battle Cards or Unisons and it gets -20000 power for the turn.” The compiler recorded only `played`, with nothing unread, so a skill removing the card did nothing.

What changed:
- `text/triggers.ts`: “when this card is **[played or]** removed from a Battle Area by a skill” (`removedFromBattle`), “… by an opponent's skill” (`removedByOpponent`) and “played or KO'd” (`koed`) each keep their half beside `played`; the card's WHEN lists both. The cause stays the printed one: a battle KO, a skill's KO and a move the rules make are not “removed … by a skill” (3-1-5).
- `playedOrUnplacedLeave` (+ `compile/index.ts`): a coordinated “played or <way of leaving play>” whose second half no trigger reader recognises is refused as an unread clause instead of becoming a bare `played`.
- 9-6-9-2: the Autos negated in the Battle Area are read before the skill's move ends the negation and travel with the `removedFromBattle` / `removedByOpponent` / `droppedFromBattle` / `droppedByOpponent` / `leftBattleToDrop` pends; a negated copy stays silent.
- Proof: `scripts/verify/played-or-removed-940.ts` (stored-row JSON round trip, real VM, both seats): play; own and opponent skill removal to Drop, hand and Warp; one question per occurrence; optional choice, -20000 and its turn duration; skill KO, plain KO, battle KO and rules moves do not trigger; negation boundary; neighbouring wordings and the unread refusal.

Known limits, left as they were:
- The same silent loss exists for other coordinated pairs: “played or attacks” (BT25-128, P-575, BT10-045, BT26-100 …) and “played or placed in your energy” (P-353) read `played` alone. They are not this family; each needs its own half taught, then the full-catalog diff.
- A Hidden Mode copy removed by a skill is not reachable through the ordinary opposing-Battle-Card selector, so 9-6-9-3 is covered by the existing declared-moment check only.

Acceptance:
- [x] Generic reading of the coordinated trigger family keeping both alternatives, the “by a skill” cause, selector, optional choice, amount and duration; no ID branch; one occurrence pends once.
- [x] Stored rows → real VM: play, own and opponent skill removal to Drop/hand/Warp, negation boundary, reload, both seats; KO and rules movement do not trigger.
- [x] An alternative the engine cannot place stays an unread gap.
- [ ] Full-catalog trigger/reading diff: needs the catalog database (`arena:diff`, not run from an agent session) — the owner runs it.
- [x] Glossary, focused checks, typecheck, lint, tests, build. No database writes.
