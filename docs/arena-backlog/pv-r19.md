Coordinate with #680's nested invocation work before touching its activation files, and with #549's flow work.

**Current boundary, source-inspected at `eb824ec`, not runtime-tested for this issue.** §12-1-3 normally means an Extra's [Activate]/[Counter] skill from hand when text says “activate an Extra Card.” §12-2-4 still declares and activates the chosen Extra skill, moves it from hand to Drop, and waives its *original* costs unless specified; it does not say the activation ceases to be an Extra activation. Ordinary zero-cost activation is also an activation. §§22-35-2 and 22-36-2 name activation of the corresponding Extra *skill*, without a payment qualifier. Hand-to-Drop `extraActivated` is already emitted for waived legacy invocation (`engine/engine.ts`, `activateLine`) and from the VM's `skillActivated(extra: true, from: hand)` moment (`vm/host.ts`/`vm/activate.ts` and `triggers.rules`). The glossary's claim that another card's effect activating an Extra cannot be this moment disagrees with that rule reading and both paths; revise it only with matching runtime evidence. An Extra activated from another area does not satisfy the default hand trigger.

The `Villainous` `afterSkill` hook in `keywords.rules` chooses a card for discard but never applies `negateOwnSkill(until: turn)`, and its comment names an already-resolved Heroic gap. `vm/activate.ts` sets `afterSkillSource` only when the activated Extra has Heroic; `vm/flow.ts` then calls `fireAfterSkill` once, so a Villainous-only Extra does not reach that hook, whether paid or invoked. In the legacy engine, `activateLine` queues `extra.finish`, which only clears `resolving`; its Heroic/Villainous pending loop is in Battle play, not Extra skill activation. The legacy loop records both under pseudo-index `-1`, and `resolveHeroicVillainous` uses `if Heroic ... else if Villainous`, so a card with both cannot receive independent payouts today. Existing `duration-shuffle-574.ts` proves invoked movement, waived price, reload and continuation, while `heroic-554.ts` and `granted-heroic-602.ts` prove paid Heroic VM paths. None establishes Villainous Extra or legacy Extra keyword payout.

**Bounded design for review after hot-file handoff.** Detect a successful qualifying Extra skill activation before the Extra moves or its effect changes its keyword, independent of whether the original cost is waived or zero. Use one post-activation effect-frame continuation and one `fireAfterSkill` capture per activation, not separate Heroic and Villainous callbacks that could duplicate an Auto. The existing `fireAfterSkill` and `hookBodiesFor` paths carry the individual printed skill index (including copied skills), timed grant effect ID, and standing grant ID into a persisted pending frame; `negateOwnSkill` uses that identity rather than a card-wide flag. `afterSkillSource` currently carries only a card ID, not an activation-time keyword snapshot: verify qualification survives any intervening trigger or source mutation before capture, while retaining the current Extra-effect-before-keyword order. Extend the legacy supported path by pending *same-keyword* Heroic/Villainous watchers once at successful Extra skill activation; avoid treating `extra.finish` as that event. The legacy `-1` pseudo-index and Heroic-first resolution need a separate identity decision for cards with both or multiple keyword instances; do not silently claim independent payouts from a card-wide mark. Correct the Villainous rule body and stale glossary/ruleset wording only alongside source and proof. Do not widen #549 Auto prices, #677 external taxes or #680's invocation eligibility in this issue.
