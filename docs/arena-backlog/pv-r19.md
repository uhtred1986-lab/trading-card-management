**Original boundary, source-inspected at `eb824ec` and frozen #680 `265affd` before this change.** §12-1-3 normally means an Extra's [Activate]/[Counter] skill from hand when text says “activate an Extra Card.” §12-2-4 still declares and activates the chosen Extra skill, moves it from hand to Drop, and waives its *original* costs unless specified; it does not say the activation ceases to be an Extra activation. Ordinary zero-cost activation is also an activation. §§22-35-2 and 22-36-2 instead name activation of the corresponding Extra *skill*, without a kind, zone or payment qualifier; §12-2-3 explicitly permits Extra [Auto]/[Activate]/[Counter] skills from another area. Thus the keyword trigger needs an audit of successful [Auto] skills as well as [Activate] and [Counter], whereas the default `extraActivated` hand trigger is narrower. Hand-to-Drop `extraActivated` is already emitted for waived legacy invocation (`engine/engine.ts`, `activateLine`) and from the VM's `skillActivated(extra: true, from: hand)` moment (`vm/host.ts`/`vm/activate.ts` and `triggers.rules`). The glossary's claim that another card's effect activating an Extra cannot be this moment disagrees with that rule reading and both paths; revise it only with matching runtime evidence. An Extra activated from another area does not satisfy the default hand trigger.

The `Villainous` `afterSkill` hook in `keywords.rules` chooses a card for discard but never applies `negateOwnSkill(until: turn)`, and its comment names an already-resolved Heroic gap. `vm/activate.ts` sets `afterSkillSource` only when the activated Extra has Heroic; `vm/flow.ts` then calls `fireAfterSkill` once, so a Villainous-only Extra does not reach that hook, whether paid or invoked. Native VM Counter activation (`vm/battle.ts`, `applyCounter`) fires `skillActivated` but never `fireAfterSkill`; legacy Counter pends `extraActivated` but not the keyword. Both engines' ordinary [Auto] paths lack an Extra keyword capture, and #549 currently owns their optional-cost sequencing. In the legacy engine, `activateLine` queues `extra.finish`, which only clears `resolving`; its Heroic/Villainous pending loop is in Battle play, not Extra skill activation. The legacy loop records both under pseudo-index `-1`, and `resolveHeroicVillainous` uses `if Heroic ... else if Villainous`, so a card with both cannot receive independent payouts today. Existing `duration-shuffle-574.ts` proves invoked movement, waived price, reload and continuation, while `heroic-554.ts` and `granted-heroic-602.ts` prove paid Heroic VM paths. None establishes Villainous Extra, native Counter/Auto or legacy Extra keyword payout.

**Bounded design for review after hot-file handoff.** Detect a successful qualifying Extra skill activation after due payment and before its effect changes the board, independent of whether the original cost is waived or zero. Snapshot source keyword qualification before a hand-to-Drop move can change it, then capture the *currently eligible watcher identities and event-only predicates* once at the successful activation boundary; do not keep only `afterSkillSource`'s card ID until a later frame. The existing `fireAfterSkill` and `hookBodiesFor` paths carry the individual printed skill index (including copied skills), timed grant effect ID, and standing grant ID into a persisted pending frame; `negateOwnSkill` uses that identity rather than a card-wide flag. Pend once, remove the deferred `vm/flow.ts` call, and let the current checkpoint drain effect programs first. Native [Counter] needs the same capture at its own successful boundary; a deferred action-price frame must still finish before the keyword payout. For [Auto], capture only after its optional cost was actually paid and its activation begun, coordinated with #549. Current `fireAfterSkill` excludes `other === source`, and in-play enumeration excludes an Extra still in hand; §22-35/36 says “another” for Battle plays but not for Extra skills. The manual does not settle here whether a source Extra's own hand keyword should answer, so preserve the exclusion as an explicit unverified boundary until a ruling or real-card proof establishes it. `vm/script.ts` resumes an invoking parent's program behind the nested Extra's effect; the pending queue waits for all programs, so verify whether keyword payout after that parent continuation matches the required checkpoint rather than assuming nested ordering. Extend the legacy supported path by pending *same-keyword* Heroic/Villainous watchers once at successful Extra skill activation; avoid treating `extra.finish` as that event. The legacy `-1` pseudo-index and Heroic-first resolution need a separate identity decision for cards with both or multiple keyword instances; do not silently claim independent payouts from a card-wide mark. Correct the Villainous rule body and stale glossary/ruleset wording only alongside source and proof. Do not widen #549 Auto prices, #677 external taxes or #680's invocation eligibility in this issue.
