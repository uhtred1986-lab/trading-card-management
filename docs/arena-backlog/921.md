Refs #907. After Earthling Token support (#919).

BT7-005 Machspeed Kaio-Ken Son Goku, BT7-029 Undying Spirit Son Gohan, BT7-058 Vegeta the Cruel and BT7-078 Champa the Trickster (Series 7, mono-colour, printed cost 3, Battle Cards with [Counter: Play]) each print “[Permanent] If a multicolored card is in your energy, reduce the energy cost of this card in your hand by 1.” The compiler refused both the condition and the dependent reduction. The same wording with colours named, or the combo cost, appears on BT20-031, EX21-16 and P-452, which were refused for the same reason.

**Owner-confirmed reading (15 Oct 2026).** The qualifying card must itself have two or more colours; two different monocolour energy cards do not satisfy the condition. The reduction is one flat 1 while at least one qualifying card is in your own Energy Area (Active or Rest Mode), never one per card, and it follows the board live.

## What changed

- `compile/conditions.ts`: “<card phrase> is/are (not) in your energy [Area]” reads as a count of at least one (or none) of that phrase in your own Energy Area. The card phrase goes through the ordinary target grammar, so “multicolored”, “Red/Blue multicolor” and the like keep their filters; the opponent's energy is not accepted as a spelling of your own.
- `compile/effects.ts`: a flat energy/combo/Z-Energy reduction of “this card in your hand” keeps the hand scope it prints (`self` in area `hand`). The plain reducer used to drop the area; only the “for each” reducer kept it.
- `glossary.ts`: the “Reducing (or raising) a cost” entry says what the condition, the flat step and the hand scope mean.
- `scripts/verify/multicolor-energy-921.ts` (registered in `verify-arena`): printed text → compile → stored-row JSON round-trip → the real VM.

## Proof (`scripts/verify/multicolor-energy-921.ts`)

For each of the four cards, on both seats (p1, p2), through the stored-row path (`skillRecords` → JSON → `rulesFromRows`) and the real VM:

- Reading: the whole [Permanent] reads, a count of at least one multicolour card in your own Energy Area, one flat `costReduction` of 1 on the card in the hand; the [Counter: Play] reads; “there is” and “your Energy Area” spellings give the same condition, the opponent's energy does not.
- One multicolour energy card, two, three, and one in Rest Mode: printed cost 3 becomes 2 (the same 2 with two or three cards).
- Two different monocolour energy cards, a multicolour card only in the opponent's energy, or only in your hand/Drop/Battle Area: cost stays 3.
- Ordinary Main Phase play and [Counter: Play] in answer to the opponent's play: the cost in hand, the price planner, the menu's payment preview and the ability row's cost (“2 energy”) all agree with the energy actually rested; a card whose discounted price cannot be paid is not offered.
- The last qualifying card leaving the Energy Area returns the cost to 3; a qualifying card coming back restores 2; the orb part of the price is unchanged; every check repeats after a JSON reload.
- Neighbours: BT20-031's opponent's-turn and 2 qualifiers, EX21-16 / P-452's combo cost, and an unconditional “reduce … this card in your hand” now scoped to the hand.

## Unverified or left open

- The catalog has no specified-cost orbs for these cards; the price planner's catalog convention (one orb per card colour, capped by the total) is unchanged and the test only asserts the orb part does not move with the discount.
- The [Auto]s of the four cards are not claimed by this issue. The engine's treatment of “it's your opponent's turn” tails was not re-audited here.
- The [Counter: Play] price on the second seat's Energy markers (the second player starts with one) was exercised: the payment options carry markers and the totals still equal the discounted cost.
