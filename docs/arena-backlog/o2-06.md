## Delivered

`scripts/arena-policy-eval.mts` (extended) is the repeatable evaluation; method, results and limits are in
`docs/arena-policy-evaluation.md`. Fixed seed sets (`scripts/verify/arena-policy-seeds.ts`): `dev` for every
number, `holdout` reserved for O5 and refused without `--allow-holdout`. Both seats on every seed; the local policy
against six baselines (`uniform`, `shortcuts`, `main646`, `combat647`, `selfplay`, `control`) on the five synthetic
fixtures (40 games each) and the four ordered SD15/SD5 matchups (20 each): 1,680 games, **0 illegal decisions, 0
interruptions or unfinished games, 0 cycle-guard trips**, every game decided. Win rates carry Wilson intervals and
sample sizes, plus seed-pair counts and seat bias read against the `control`. Hidden-state twins (2,280 positions
sampled) gave the same observation, answer and a playable move every time; replays were identical. Latency per
window in Node, and in a module Worker and a 4x-throttled main thread of headless Chrome
(`npm run arena:policy-browser`: the browser plays the same games as Node, decision hash for hash).
`npm run arena:policy-eval -- --check` (about 15 s) is in `npm test` before `ai-agent-sdk.ts`.

Headline: against `uniform` 85 % (79-89 %, n=200) on the fixtures and 94 % (86-97 %, n=80) on the audit decks;
against the #647 policy 56 % (50-63 %) and 55 % (44-65 %): no measurable gain from #650. Seat bias is
fixture-specific (641 is decided by the seat; `control` first seat 100 %), absent on the audit decks. Limits
(synthetic fixtures, two starter decks, no real-player benchmark, a simulated phone) are in the doc.
