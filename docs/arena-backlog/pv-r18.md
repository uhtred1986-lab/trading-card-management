**Source:** [Issue #677](https://github.com/uhtred1986-lab/trading-card-management/issues/677); Rule Manual v4.00 §20-14 and §12-2-4. This is the external-tax gap found while implementing SD7-01 under #574, separate from its original-cost waiver.

**Delivery update (branch `arena-677-activate-skill-tax`, Refs #677).** A synthetic wording, "your opponent can't activate skills unless they send 1 card from their hand to their Warp each time", now compiles to `forbid(activateSkill, side: opponent, unlessPay: { choose(hand, notSelf) moveTo(warp) })` and validates (`TAXABLE` gains `activateSkill`); narrower descriptions, card subjects, non-"each time" and other-payer forms stay unread. Both engines read the taxes once at declaration (before counted uses are spent), refuse an activation whose set of taxes cannot be paid whole (with the priced `forbidden` refusal and a "— first pay" label), and queue one price frame per tax in front of the activation's counter window, effect and invoking parent — on declared Main/Battle text lines and on `invokeSkill` (SD7-01, BT31-116), whose printed-cost waiver does not waive the tax. A keyword's own activation move, an [Auto] (incl. Heroic/Villainous hooks) and a [Counter] under a tax stop with typed #677 `NotYet` on both engines. An exact `choose` answered with a no-longer-eligible card is now refused rather than read as an empty pick. Proof: `scripts/verify/activate-tax-677.ts` and the flipped `duration-shuffle-574.ts` block. Judgement: the taxes owed are fixed at declaration (20-14-1), so a rule whose source leaves mid-payment is still paid once and a rule newly in force mid-payment is not added. Open: no printed catalog card is claimed; Auto/Counter/keyword-move charging; saved-session DB revision/CAS for this path is not separately exercised.

**Baseline boundary (before the delivery update), source-inspected.** `scripts/verify/duration-shuffle-574.ts` injects a *typed effect* with `forbid: { what: "activateSkill", pay: ... }`. Both engines' `taxesOn(..., "activateSkill", ...)` readers find that injected effect, but normal and SD7-01-invoked activation omit its price; the scenario asserts that omission as a gap. It does **not** prove a printed card text compiles to a playable activation tax. `compile/effects.ts` recognises payable clauses only for attack, play and switching energy Active; `vm/script-schema.ts`'s `TAXABLE` and validation reject `unlessPay` for `activateSkill`. A bounded synthetic wording can verify the compiler/schema and engine path; real printed catalog coverage remains unclaimed without a matching card/ruling. Original Extra-cost waiver in §12-2-4 does not establish a waiver of an external 20-14 tax.

**Build.** First compile and validate a bounded synthetic activation-tax wording without widening another prohibition; claim catalog support only when a real printed case is confirmed. Charge each applicable external tax once on normal Main/Battle and effect-invoked activations. A tax is due at the *successful* activation boundary, before the activated skill's own effects and triggers; an unaffordable or declined tax must not leave an Extra moved, an activation spent or a parent invocation advanced. Reuse native payment choices and bindings, but preserve the invoking effect's nested continuation across a deferred price prompt and JSON reload. Re-evaluate the ordered, live set after each paid tax: current readers return only ops/display name/duration, not a stable source/effect identity, so a saved choice must not double-charge a rule, skip a newly due rule, or charge one whose source has left. A `forbid(what: activateSkill)` can also govern [Auto] and [Counter] skill activations under §§9-6/9-7; audit those gates and coordinate #549's optional Auto-cost path rather than silently calling a Main/Battle-only fix universal. Original-cost waiver must not silently waive external taxes. Interrupt an unmodelled due price visibly before treating the effect as resolved.
