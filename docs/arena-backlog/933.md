Refs #907. BT13-017 SSB Son Goku, at Full Power; BT13-021 SSB Vegeta, at Full Power and BT13-024 SS Broly, Brawn Amplified share “[Permanent] This card can't be played from any area with non-[Evolve] skills.” The compiler left the line unread, so nothing stopped a plain skill play of them.

Owner interpretation (issue body): ordinary play from hand for the printed five energy stays legal; a play through a non-Evolve skill is prohibited whatever the source area; Evolve, EX-Evolve and effects that explicitly instruct the card to evolve stay eligible. The classification is by what the play is, not by the keyword tag on the activating skill (P-336's evolution is an [Activate: Main]).

Delivered:
- Compiler: the Permanent reads as `forbid(what: play, bySkill: true, exceptEvolve: true)` on the card itself, shared, no card ids. P-336's “evolve it on top of this card in Active Mode” reads as `play(onto: this card, mode: active, using: Evolve)`, which is also the form any other skill's explicit evolution takes.
- VM: the `play` op asks 20-14 with `evolution` (the play's own `using`); `exceptEvolve` rules let evolutions through. A declared play now runs with `declaredPlay` and is asked as `bySkill: false`, so a “can't be played by skills” card is no longer refused when the player plays it normally.
- Proof: `scripts/verify/non-evolve-play-933.ts` (both seats, all three cards, stored-row JSON round trip and reload): five-energy play with the two red orbs, four or blue energy refused; EX-Evolve on a valid base, invalid base refused; plain skill play from hand, Drop and Warp and a bare “on top of” play refused with nothing moved or duplicated, a forged choice refused; P-336 evolves BT13-024 in Active Mode; an [Auto] that evolves does the same; negated skills lift the restriction.

Not delivered / explicit dependencies:
- A printed [EX-Evolve] whose price adds “if you have 3 or more energy and you draw 1 card” is refused as `unread` today with or without this restriction (control in the proof); the EX-Evolve proof here uses `{r}{r}{r}` alone. Fixing the keyword's price is separate work.
- P-336's search Auto is outside this issue.
- Choosing a restricted card for a skill play is not narrowed (choosing is not playing); the play then does nothing.
- Live rule refresh after deployment is for the owner/root as a guarded supported-script step; no live record was written.
