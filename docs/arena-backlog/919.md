## Problem
BT23-078/080/082/096 share “[Permanent] For each of your Earthling Tokens, reduce the energy cost of this card in your hand by 1.” The compiler currently splits the multiplier away and emits a flat reduction of one alongside an unread fragment. This must become a shared live count, not four overrides.

Trunks & Mai (BT23-071, PERFECT COMBINATION) ties the deck together: green Mai: Future Battle Cards costing1 can be fetched; BT23-087/088 rest to create Earthling Tokens under its green dual-character Leader condition. BT23-074 is a Z-Battle whose play Auto also creates a token. The Leader awakens with three tokens and conditionally grants them Barrier. Actual token instances already exist in the engine, with mode/owner/encoded definition and board rendering; do not add a separate numeric counter or infer token presence from a Leader.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Compile all four full printed Permanents into one count-based hand-only reducer; no leftover flat discount when the leading multiplier is unread or malformed.
- [ ] Count own Earthling Token instances in Battle, Active or Rest; exclude opposing tokens, other token names, ordinary Earthling-trait cards, and tokens outside Battle. Update dynamically on creation/removal. Preserve standard total/specified cost layers and floor; do not invent missing catalog specified orbs.
- [ ] Prove real token creation/identity, Leader-qualified support, three-token Awaken threshold, conditional Barrier and reload/board visibility through existing paths. Document any discovered dependency rather than claiming full-deck readiness.
- [ ] Audit representative other token Leaders (Ghost, Chilled’s Army, Clone, Cell Jr./Multi-Form) against existing generic tracking and list independent gaps without promising blanket support.
- [ ] Focused compiled-record-to-VM proof, compiler reading/corpus delta reviewed, glossary and public evidence; typecheck, lint, full tests and build.

## Scope and boundaries
Reuse token instances, token filters, dynamic Amount/count and continuous cost layers. No production data write or approval. No UI redesign or automatic Leader counters. Known separate unread dependencies: BT23-071 back attack Auto’s both-players total10 Battle Cards bonus; BT23-088 on-play search. Review and report, do not silently clear these gaps.
