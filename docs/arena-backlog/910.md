## Problem and verified reproduction
The seven BT25 Wish Z-Unisons share:

> [Permanent] This card can't attack and gains ≪Shenron≫ in all areas.

Affected cards: BT25-138 Son Goku, Accidental Wish; BT25-139 Son Goku Vs. Frieza, Final Battle Wish; BT25-140 Krillin, Wish After Conflict; BT25-141 Oolong, Split-Second Wish; BT25-142 Zamasu, Scheme Wish; BT25-143 Android 17, Wish for Restored Universes; BT25-144 Cheelai, Wish for the Fighting to End.

At current main, compiling the isolated sentence “This card gains ≪Shenron≫ in all areas” produces `gains` with trait `shenron` and `allAreas: true`, without unread clauses. Compiling the actual joined text produces only `forbid attack` and leaves “gains ≪Shenron≫ in all areas” unread. This is an observed shared wording gap, not seven independent rulings or a request to rebuild the Wish mechanic. A parser repair must still prove real trait behavior through the engine.
