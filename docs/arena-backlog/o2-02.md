## Delivered

The rules moved, not copied: `src/lib/arena/policy/shortcuts.ts` holds one-legal-move, first player,
mulligan, charge, Z-Energy and Empower as pure functions of a `ShortcutInput` — what a local policy
has (`shortcutInputOf(PolicyInput)`: the #644 observation and decisions) and nothing else. Ties go to
the first of the tied decisions in the order listed; no RNG is drawn (`shortcutThen(fallback)` hands
the policy RNG on untouched). `src/lib/arena/ai/shortcut-input.ts` is the online adapter
(`freeChoice`): it reads either engine's state through `zoneOf`/`leaderOf`/`catalogDefOf`, lists the
caller's menu in the engine's order as the decisions, and maps the chosen id back to its index.
`opponent.ts` lost `freeChoice`/`chargeChoice`/`mulliganChoice` and keeps its provider path,
fallback and accounting as they were.

- Golden cases: `arena-policy-shortcuts.mts` section 5 — empty menu in every window, no matching
  shortcut, ties either way round, thresholds (two cards costing <= 2; an X cost is 9 to charge and
  not cheap to keep), off-colour +10, the decline never chosen while a card can be charged, the words.
- Provider path: section 6 — with no provider configured every one of the 1,185 shortcut decisions
  answers through `chooseMove` with no spend; with a (fake) provider they make no request and a
  non-shortcut Main Phase still makes exactly one and is accounted.
- Hidden data and game RNG: section 4 — at all 3,153 positions, rotating the identities of every card
  the seat cannot read, reversing the decks and moving the game's seed and RNG leaves the online and
  the contract choice unchanged; the policy RNG comes back untouched from a shortcut and
  `shortcutThen(uniformPolicy)` is reproducible and seed-dependent where it falls through.
- Equivalence: `fixtures/arena-policy-shortcuts/before.json` was recorded from origin/main's
  `freeChoice` (f9d3868a) before the move; the adapter picks the same index and words at all 1,185
  positions that had a shortcut and declines at the other 1,968. Through `policyTurn` the same rule
  picks the same engine move at all 878 pinned positions (menu in the engine's order); in the
  contract's canonical id order 69 positions pick another of two tied moves (never anything else).

Judgement calls: legacy `mulliganed` is not read (a seat is prompted once, so it was always false);
the contract's `pick` decision is not a shortcut candidate; a menu holding a non-menu action
(`refereeRuling`) is only seen by the online adapter (the referee never reaches `chooseMove`).

Not run: `npm run contract:emit` and `arena:fuzz -- 40` (no engine or rules change); `npm test`'s
`ai-agent-sdk.ts` fails on Windows (POSIX `/tmp`) and the rest of the chain was run by hand.
