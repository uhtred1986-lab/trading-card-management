## Shared printed skill and scope

Five cards print:

> [Auto][Once per turn] When your opponent switches their energy to Active Mode using a [Counter] skill, place up to 1 card in your opponent's life and 1 card in your opponent's energy in their Drop Area.

DB3-013 Mai, King Piccolo's Underling; DB3-040 Borgos, Bardock's Crewmate; DB3-059 Vegeta, Protecting His Loved Ones; DB3-089 Chi-Chi, Melee Matriarch; DB3-105 Son Gohan, Changing History.

Official basis (checked 17 Oct 2026): the Bandai card list prints the line on all five (the [Counter] icon is an image, `Counter.png`); the official card Q&A (`card_faq.php`) and the errata list (`errata-cards.php`) carry **no entry** for any of the five IDs, so none is invented. Manual 5-2-4 (up to N is 0..N), 5-10 (damage is not plain area movement), 9-6, 22-11 ([Once per turn] per copy).

## Owner-confirmed interpretation (locked during voice review)

A real opponent [Counter] skill readies the opponent's own rested energy. The controller of this Auto chooses zero or one opposing Life card and zero or one opposing energy card and places them directly in the opponent's Drop Area. The opponent does not choose and cannot decline. At most one from each area per activation, however many energy were readied. [Once per turn] per copy. Life-to-Drop is not damage and not Life-to-hand.

## What was built

- `modeSwitched` moments carry `kind: counter` when the switching frame resolves an activated [Counter] (`ScriptFrame.skillKind`, set in `vm/battle.ts` for the Counter effect, its price frames and its alternative-payment frame; carried to a tax's price frame). Typed, never read from the source card's other lines.
- Trigger `opponentOwnEnergyActiveByCounter` (`triggers.rules`, `types.ts`, `gaps.ts`, `text/triggers.ts`): `modeSwitched(byOpponent: false, kind: counter, in: energy, mode: active, watcher: opponent)`; no turn condition.
- Compound placement reading (`compile/effects.ts`): "place up to 1 card in your opponent's life and 1 card in your opponent's energy in their Drop Area" becomes two optional choices and two plain moves to the Drop; the "up to" holds for both. The Life choice is `blind` (face-down Life is offered by position).
- `vm/triggers.ts`: a free [Once per turn] Auto already waiting for the same moment is not pended twice (several energy readied at once).
- `vm/view.ts`, `vm/index.ts`: a blind choice exposes no face in `choices` and its menu labels are positional.
- Proof: `scripts/verify/counter-energy-916.ts` (suite `counter-energy-916`).
