**Source:** #788 spec §7, "Engine dependency" (https://github.com/uhtred1986-lab/trading-card-management/issues/788#issuecomment-6171895727). **Tracker:** #795. Blocks UX 5 (play: cost and targets before commit).

## Why
The S21 playtest: a card with an on-play KO skill could be played when nothing could be targeted, and the player only found out afterwards (#783 added a notice *after* the fact). The approved design warns **before** commit: "You can play this card — but its skill has no target", with the reason, and a **Play anyway** button. That fact has to come from the engine, not the UI.

## What exists
`view.you.hand[].abilities[]` already carries `availability`, and the card inspector shows "no target" for some abilities (e.g. "Grow Unison … Nothing to choose: no Unison Card in play to grow"). On the play action there is nothing yet that says whether the card's **on-play** ([Auto] when played) skill would find a legal choice.

## Build
- For each legal `play` action, the snapshot states whether the card's on-play skill would have at least one legal choice at that moment. Where it would not, it gives a short reason a player can read (e.g. "no red Unison Card in play"), and where it would, which cards are candidates.
- Both engines must answer the same way (see `docs/arena-tooling.md`). Before choosing the field, verify where `legal` and `abilities` are built and what the contract version policy is (`docs/arena-client-contract.md`, `contract:emit`). Don't guess the path.
- "Up to N" skills stay playable; this is information, never a block.

## Acceptance
- [x] Scripted cases on the current rules engine (the only engine since #118): Vegeta, Saiyan Youth (BT11-011) with no red Unison Card in play reports "no target" with its reason; with one, it reports the candidate. Transcendent Strike (BT6-025) lists only Battle Cards at 20,000 or less without [Barrier].
- [x] `npx tsx scripts/verify-arena.ts`, functional `npm test` checks, and the `contract:emit` review if the snapshot shape changes (local timing recorded separately per owner instruction). Glossary updated if the compiler's reading changes (CLAUDE.md rule).
