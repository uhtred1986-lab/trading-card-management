Refs #907. Priority P1: a card is offered more cheaply or more often than printed because a clause of its price was dropped without a word.

BT19-145 prints “[Activate: Battle]{u}/{y}, if your Leader is blue or yellow and 3 or more blue and/or yellow cards are in your Combo Area: Play this card from your hand.” and read only “if your Leader is blue or yellow”: the Combo Area count was lost. P-441 prints “[Activate: Main]{u}, if your Leader is blue, has <Gotenks> in its character name, and you send 1 blue card with <Son Goten> … and 1 blue card with <Trunks> … from your hand and/or Drop to your Warp: Play this card from your hand.” and read only “if your Leader is blue”: the Gotenks requirement and the send-to-Warp cost were lost, so the card was played for {u} alone.

Root cause: the Leader branch of `parseConditionClause` (`your leader is (.+)`) hands its whole greedy tail to `parseFilter`, which keeps what it recognises and ignores the rest; `allConditions` falls back to that whole-text read whenever a piece does not parse; and a printed price that was neither a condition nor an action `compileAction` could read produced no price at all and no unread line.

Acceptance:
- [x] No silent partial reading. A Leader description ends where a verb, a second subject or a counted clause begins; “and/or” across places is refused; a price that reads as neither condition nor chargeable action leaves the whole line unread (`priceIsUnread`).
- [x] Supported clauses are read fully: a Leader property list (“is blue, has <Gotenks> in its character name”), “N or more … are in your Combo Area”, “blue and/or yellow” as one either-colour filter, and the price proper behind a [Bond] / [Spirit Boost] descriptor.
- [x] BT19-145 and P-441: printed text → compile → stored-row JSON round-trip → real VM (`scripts/verify/price-condition-clauses.ts`): BT19-145 is offered only with three qualifying Combo Area cards; P-441 (two-card send price) is unread and never offered.
- [x] Full-catalog reading delta reviewed (in the PR); glossary updated (“A price is read whole or not at all”).

Left unread, not read wrongly (follow-ups): multi-card send/place prices (“send 1 <A> card and 1 <B> card … to your Warp”), a Leader-or-property disjunction (“is green or has <GT> in its character name”), the “—both yellow—” qualifier after a dash, “a total of N … and/or …” counts, and “you either have … or …”.

**Source:** `src/lib/arena/compile/conditions.ts`, `src/lib/arena/compile/prices.ts`, [Bandai card list BT19-145 and P-441](https://www.dbs-cardgame.com/us-en/cardlist/index.php?search=true).
