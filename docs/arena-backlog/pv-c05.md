**Source:** [#680](https://github.com/uhtred1986-lab/trading-card-management/issues/680), the remaining BT31-116 effect in parent [#539](https://github.com/uhtred1986-lab/trading-card-management/issues/539), and Rule Manual v4.00 §12-2-4. [#571](https://github.com/uhtred1986-lab/trading-card-management/issues/571) delivered the qualifying trigger and subject filter; its scoped issue remains closed.

**Defect.** BT31-116 used to compile its hand Extra effect as `play`, putting an Extra into the Battle Area instead of activating its [Activate: Main/Battle] skill. Its White Cell/Cell Jr. cost qualification was also checked only in the Auto's later effect, after the Auto price and after other pending Autos could change the played card. The owner ruled the chosen Extra's original energy and skill costs free; Rule Manual v4.00 §§9-6-2, 12-1-3 and 12-2-4 define the play moment and nested activation.

**Delivery.** The exact printed Cell trigger qualifies its played subject at event time, before the Auto price is offered, using current energy cost after in-force reductions. A free-play alternative changes payment, not the card's energy cost. The VM pays BT31-116's own Revealed-to-Hidden energy price, then the player may choose one qualifying mono-white cost-2 hand Extra and activate its real Main/Battle line. The card goes to Drop before that skill resolves. Its original costs are waived; legality conditions, once limits, prohibitions and the ordinary hand Extra activation moment remain. SD7-01's separate Desire Main invocation retains its own selector and cost bound.
