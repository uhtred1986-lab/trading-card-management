Source: official comprehensive rules22-13, https://www.dbs-cardgame.com/pdf/rulemanual.pdf?ver_4.00 ; https://www.dbs-cardgame.com/us-en/rule/card_faq.php ; https://www.dbs-cardgame.com/us-en/rule/keyword-skills.php .

vm/activate.ts actionPriceOf returns null whenever line.keyword exists, while many Union-Fusion/Potara cards print draw/other action costs before the colon. Examples BT25-060 Draw1 with no energy orbs; BT11-013 Draw2 +red3; BT20-125 send opponent Battle to Warp and draw1; BT26-084/085 draw1 and mill opponent top card. compileSkillText deliberately skips Fusion/Potara descriptions, so unread=0 is not evidence the costs execute. The full chargeablePrice gate refuses keyword action-price records (except Aegis/Swap) as unread; this is unsupported activation, not proof that the card is played for free.

Audit every Union activation's cost, condition, Burst/Limit and reductions. Build a supported staged payment: Fusion material reveal and Drop payment precedes determining/paying additional cost (22-13-4-2/-4/-4-1), then activation/resolution. Cost draws are costs, including deck-failure and action-payment checkpoints. Do not treat cost as optional effect or silently ignore it. Preserve correct payment colors/alternative orbs, Leader/energy conditions, once/Limit consumption, privacy and serialization. Runtime tests from complete printed records, not hand-authored corrected programs.

Acceptance: focused printed-text/runtime regression proof, glossary and corpus-delta review, typecheck/lint/full tests/build. No live database writes. Keep unrelated gaps open.

## Resolution

One shared mechanic for the action a keyword move prints before its colon, on [Union-Fusion], [Union-Potara] and the [Evolve] family (the EX-Evolve draw cost found during #933):

- **Reading** (`compile/prices.ts`): the price before the colon of a Union-Fusion/Potara or Evolve-family line is compiled as a price program, as [Swap]'s has been since #729 (the owning keyword tag comes off for the price only). The orb notation "{b}/{w}" is stripped like any other orb (BT29-141, BT22-140). A Leader description no longer swallows an action after a comma ("your Leader is a green <Zamasu> card, you draw 1 card", BT26-084), and a misprinted action ("and your draw 3 cards", BT31-149) refuses the price as unread instead of leaving its condition alone — which had offered BT31-149 with no draw at all.
- **Paying** (`vm/keyword-costs.ts`, `payActionPrice()` in `keywords.rules`): a cost, on the strict action-price host shared with Auto/Aegis/Counter/Swap/Drop. Offered only while the energy the plan pays leaves the whole action payable (5-8-3; a draw needs that many cards in the deck); paid where the `DO` says — Fusion after its materials are in the Drop (22-13-4-4-1), Potara and Evolve first (22-13-5-2, 22-5-3); announced and its moments fired once paid; a price that can no longer be paid fails the activation with what was paid kept (5-8-4). A shape outside the strict host's families, or a Fusion price that could touch its materials, is a typed `NotYet` naming #926. The strict host learned "draw N" (opt-in for this family only).
- **Catalog**: `errata.ts` corrects BT31-149's "your draw 3 cards" to the official "you draw 3 cards".

Proof: `scripts/verify/keyword-action-costs-926.ts` (in `verify-arena`), both seats, every step through a JSON reload.

Offline corpus delta over `evidence/923/inventory.json` (114 Fusion/Potara activations): 39 lines move from refused-as-unread to an action price charged (24 bare, 15 with a printed condition); BT22-140 moves from unread to orbs-and-condition; BT31-149's catalog text moves from condition-only (played free of its draw) to unread, and to charged once the errata correction is synced; 58 orb-only and 13 condition-only lines are unchanged.

The two compiler guards are shared, so the offline audit lines (`docs/rules-audit-2026-10-15/audit.json`, 1,985 lines, plus the 923 inventory's cards: 2,604 records) show 12 changes outside Fusion/Potara, all from a price that had been read in part: seven printed [Activate] prices now charge the action the comma had hidden (EX23-11 ×2 the hand card to the deck's bottom, BT23-076 the three Earthling Tokens rested, BT30-130 the discard, EX23-12 the three Z-Energy, EX23-49 the card sent to the Warp, EX24-27 the chosen <Frieza: Br>, P-309 this card discarded); BT23-132 and P-616 were offered on their condition alone and are now unread; P-327 ([Union-Absorb], "{g}/{y}, if …") gains its condition; BT5-120 ([EX-Evolve], "Place all cards from your hand other than this card in your Drop Area") is now a charged price (reading only — not exercised at runtime here).

Not done here: [Union-Absorb] action prices (still refused, #930); energy is charged at declaration, before the Fusion materials move — no material area is the energy area, so only the log order differs from 22-13-4-4-1's letter; a [Limit]/once-per-turn use is spent at declaration, so a price that fails later (5-8-4) keeps it spent. The Evolve-family corpus outside BT13-017/021/024 is not in an offline inventory; its delta needs the live catalog.
