Audit every Union activation's cost, condition, Burst/Limit and reductions. Build a supported staged payment: Fusion material reveal and Drop payment precedes determining/paying additional cost (22-13-4-2/-4/-4-1), then activation/resolution. Cost draws are costs, including deck-failure and action-payment checkpoints. Do not treat cost as optional effect or silently ignore it. Preserve correct payment colors/alternative orbs, Leader/energy conditions, once/Limit consumption, privacy and serialization. Runtime tests from complete printed records, not hand-authored corrected programs.

Acceptance: focused printed-text/runtime regression proof, glossary and corpus-delta review, typecheck/lint/full tests/build. No live database writes. Keep unrelated gaps open.
