Refs #907. Priority P1: real-game correctness; preserve owner-authored stored rows.

EX03-14 **Last One Standing Son Goku** prints: “When you play this card from your hand, if there are 9 or more ≪Universe 7≫ in your Drop Area, for the duration of the turn, this card gains +10000 power and [Triple Strike], and its attacks can't be negated.” The protected stored owner program triggers on any play and supplies only power/Triple Strike. Official Bandai FAQ: attack negation is not attack prevention (“Battle Cards can't attack” still stops this card), and the nine-card count includes TB1-095, which gains ≪Universe 7≫ in all areas.

What changed (shared mechanics, no card-ID branch):
- `playedFromHand` (`types.ts`, `gaps.ts`, `triggers.rules` + `files.ts`, `text/triggers.ts`): “when you play this card from your hand” / “when this card is played from your hand” is `moved(asPlay: true, from: hand, to: [battle, unison])`, the origin the move itself captured. A normal play and a skill's play from the hand answer it; a play from the Drop, Warp or deck does not. Only the bare qualifier is read; “from your hand or Warp”, “from your deck or hand”, “… during your opponent's turn” stay a plain `played`.
- `permit` gains `what: "attackUnnegatable"` (`types.ts`, `script.ts`, `script-schema.ts`, `effects.ts`): timed (a resolved skill's effect) or standing ([Permanent]). `compile/effects.ts` reads “its attack(s) / this card's attack / that card's attack can't/cannot be negated [for the turn]”; “attack and skill cannot be negated” is cut into the attack rule and the existing 9-1-5 skill rule (`taxWordings`). Old programs stay valid (an enum value was added).
- VM (`host.ts`, `script-host.ts`, `script.ts`): `negateAttack()` returns whether it negated. A protected attacker's attack stays; no `did.negateAttack` / `negateLeaderAttack` is recorded, so “if you negated” reports failure, and the rest of the Counter still resolves. A ban on attacking and removal of the attacker are not negation and are untouched.
- `vm/effects.ts` (`permanents`): “gains ≪X≫ in all areas” was read only in the areas a card is played from, so TB1-095 did not count in the Drop. Such an identity change is now read in every area, found by a cheap printed-text test on cards outside the usual zones.
- `vm/triggers.ts`, `vm/choice-preview.ts`, `probe.ts`: a record that says `placed` + `playedFromHand` answers one arrival once; the choice preview and the probe know the new moment.
- Proof: `scripts/verify/attack-negation-936.ts` (printed text → compile → stored-row JSON round trip → real VM, both seats): nine / eight-plus-all-area / eight; hand play and skill-mediated play from hand, Drop, Warp and deck (with a plain-`played` control); a Counter that negates (own draw resolves, “if you negated” draw does not, no negation event, damage dealt) against the unprotected control; “can't attack” still stops the protected card; a Counter that removes the attacker; expiry at turn end; reload on every action; a [Permanent] variant.

Wording inventory (offline: `docs/rules-audit-2026-10-15/audit.json`; no database):
- Timed, on the card itself: EX03-14 “its attacks can't be negated” (for the duration of the turn).
- Timed, on a chosen card: BT25-126 “that card's attack can't be negated for the turn”.
- [Permanent]: P-080 “This card's attack cannot be negated”; BT1-043 and TB1-097 “This card's attack and skill cannot be negated”; P-072 “When your opponent has 4 or more Battle Cards in play, this card's attack can't be negated” (conditional, same op).
- Explicit attack-prevention wording is the existing `forbid what: attack` (“can't attack”, #937) and is not the same op.
- Skill-negation immunity (“This card's skills can't be negated in any area”, BT11-147; DB3-028 “This skill can't be negated in any area”) is 9-1-5 `forbid beNegated`, also separate.
- Hand-qualified plays: about 41 audited skills print “when you play this card / this card is played from your hand” (BT8-122, BT10-020, …); 7 more name a wider origin or timing (“or attacks”, “from your deck or hand”, “from your hand or Warp”, “during your opponent's turn”) and are left as they were.

Known limits, left as they were:
- Stored compiler-owned and owner rows are not rewritten. A row that says `played` keeps answering every origin, and EX03-14's protected program keeps its two omissions until an owner-reviewed, guarded refresh swaps it for the reading above (proposed program in the PR).
- The wider origin lists above stay a plain `played`.
- BT25-126's “+10000 power for each color type on all cards in your energy and Battle Area” is read by the existing counting rules, not changed here.
