Refs #907. Related #913.

The printed front Auto at skill index 20 of **TB3-019 Final Strike Bardock** is:

> [Auto] When this card is switched from Rest Mode to Active Mode, if your Leader Card is a ≪Bardock's Crew≫ card, your opponent chooses 1 card in their hand and places it at the bottom of their deck.

Before: `skillRecords` returned an empty `trigger`, no `unread`, and the program “opponent discards 1, then move your Leader to the bottom of your deck” under the Leader trait: a clean parse of the wrong card. #913's positive control (a Rest-to-Active switch under a Bardock's Crew Leader) could therefore not fire it.

## Official evidence (checked 17 Oct 2026)
- Card list (https://www.dbs-cardgame.com/us-en/cardlist/index.php?free=TB3-019&search=true) prints the line above.
- Card Q&A (https://www.dbs-cardgame.com/us-en/rule/card_faq.php): the only TB3-019 entry is Q1 (Mafuba on top, a second {BT3-077 Evil Psyche Zamasu}: [Unique]) and does not concern this Auto. **DB3-027 Q1**: evolving via the energy-base [EX-Evolve] over a Rest Mode TB3-019 in energy does **not** activate this Auto. No other Auto-specific ruling exists.
- Errata (https://www.dbs-cardgame.com/us-en/rule/errata-cards.php): no entry for TB3-019.
- Manual: 1-10-1 (modes), 0-2-4-1 (a card already in a mode does not switch), 5-2 (choices), 9-1-3-1 (skills are not valid in the Energy Area), 22-5-3-1-1 (the preliminary base move fires no trigger).

## Acceptance
- [x] **Exact Rest-to-Active self-transition**: new trigger `selfRestToActive` (`ON modeSwitched(mode: active, in: [battle, leader, unison])`, `BIND "self"`), matched only by the full wording “when this card is switched from Rest Mode to Active Mode”. A card already Active fires no `modeSwitched` (host and Active Phase both skip it); an arrival (play, energy-base evolution) is a move and fires none; a negated Auto does not answer; the Energy Area is out. The Active Phase's rule-made stand-up now carries its area (`in`) like a skill's switch, so it matches too; patterns that need a cause (`by`, `byOpponent`, `kind`) still do not match it.
- [x] **Whole Auto**: Leader trait condition kept; `choose` 1 card of the opponent's own hand, chooser the opponent; the same variable moves to the bottom of the opponent's deck. No Drop discard, no Leader move. The clause pair is read together in `compile/effects.ts` (the plain “chooses N cards in their hand” stays a `discard` when followed by anything else, and the Drop wording is unchanged).
- [x] **Real VM proof** (`scripts/verify/final-strike-bardock-922.ts`, both seats, stored-row JSON round trip, state reloaded at every step): positive by a skill's switch and by the Active Phase stand-up; two distinguishable opposing hand cards, either one chosen; destination and order asserted (chosen card last, rest of the deck unmoved, other hand card kept, Drop/Leader/own deck untouched). Negatives: wrong Leader, opponent's Leader qualifying instead, Active-to-Active repeat, negation, Energy Area, empty hand, the opponent's own Bardock.
- [x] **Official pairing**: DB3-027's energy-base evolution over the Rest Mode TB3-019 asks nobody and emits no mode event for the base; a plain arrival is silent; the same Bardock restood for real asks.
- [x] **Privacy / chooser**: only the opponent is asked, from exactly its two hand cards; neither snapshot nor policy observation exposes the opposing hand identities (menu answers resolve to current legal actions).
- [x] **Glossary** updated; reading delta below.
- [ ] Later live refresh: exact card/face/skill, guarded and separately reviewed after deployment; not part of this change. No database was touched.

## Reading delta (frozen catalog, `arena-readings --unread`, before vs after)
Four skills changed, all the same printed sentence “your opponent chooses 1 card in their hand and places it at the bottom of their deck”:

| Skill | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| TB3-019#20 | trigger none; opponent discards 1, move your Leader to deck | trigger `selfRestToActive`; choose 1 of the opponent's hand, move it to deck |
| BT16-139#20 | opponent discards 1, move this card to deck | choose 1 of the opponent's hand, move the chosen card to deck |
| BT9-099#20 | opponent discards 1, move this card to deck | same repair |
| BT5-044#10 | opponent discards 1, unread “places it at the bottom of their deck” | choose 1 of the opponent's hand, move it to deck (its “when your opponent plays a Battle Card” trigger stays unread, as before) |

## Not changed
`BT-` cards that print “when this card is switched to Active Mode” (no “from Rest Mode”, or with a named skill) keep their readings. Existing stored rows are not rewritten; a guarded refresh after deployment is the owner's.
