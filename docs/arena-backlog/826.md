# Improve resource-aware opponent search and re-evaluate on fresh holdouts

Follow-up to #824/#663/#664 under #637. The first bounded search passed legality/privacy/budget checks but scored 41–39 over 80 held-out games and failed its predeclared strength gate. Preserve this negative result and the existing heuristic default.

## Concrete next experiment

The frozen three-attack tactical fixture exposes a missing resource effect: ordinary Leader damage moves Life cards into the defender’s hand, changing later combo resources. The approximate search also omits the attacker’s own combo decisions. Model those resources from the permitted observation and sampled hidden possibilities, then evaluate whether deeper attack search is worth its cost. Never read the actual hidden hand/Life/deck.
