## Problem

`canPayPriceProgram` (`src/lib/arena/vm/activate.ts`, around line 1023) returns false for any activation price the static planner refuses. The refusal is `activationPriceProblem` in `src/lib/arena/vm/activation-price-support.ts`: a `moveTo` whose `to` is `under` or `play` is refused with "the activation payment planner cannot charge moveTo under". Callers see that as "cannot pay: <cost>" (`activate.ts`, `activationRefusals`, around line 590), and the same check guards the 20-14-1 `unlessPay` path in `src/lib/arena/vm/actions.ts` (around line 1199). The line is therefore never offered, even when the price is affordable.

Reference card: **BT10-091 One-Star Ball, Parasitic Darkness, front 20** `[Activate: Main]`: "place this card under it: play ... Frieza: Xeno on top". Engine proof (20 Oct 2026): with a cost-4 `<Frieza>` in the Battle Area and a Xeno in hand, the line still reads "cannot pay".

The batch-3 receipt says the same refusal covers **133 stored Activate rows**. Those rows mix several price shapes (place-under, KO as price, `comboFrom`, shuffle, reveal). This issue takes only the place-under shape, because it is the one the reference card needs and the one whose payment is a plain move of a card the price chose.

## Source of truth

- Rule Manual 23-2 (`docs/rules/rulemanual.txt`): cards placed under other cards, and 23-2-1 on which areas allow it.
- Rule Manual 1-2 (energy costs) and 4-3-3 (an action price in front of an [Activate] line, as the glossary describes it, #458).
- The printed text of BT10-091 front 20.
- Official FAQ for this shape: not checked in this issue. If the FAQ contradicts the manual, the FAQ wins (owner's order, `docs/arena-backlog.md` section 5).

## Scope of this issue

1. Make `moveTo` to `under` a payable step of an activation price when the target is the card the price chose (a bound `choose` variable), and the destination is that chosen card's pile.
2. Keep `play` refused. Keep KO as price, `comboFrom`, shuffle and reveal refused, with their existing reasons. They need an owner ruling on payment (which card, which area, whether the payer may decline) and are a follow-up, not part of this change.
3. Update the glossary entry for activation prices (`src/lib/arena/glossary.ts`, the costs entry) in the same commit, so its `support` claim matches the code.

## Out of scope

- Editing any `card_rules` row, including BT10-091 front 20. Rules are records: the engine must read the row as it is. No errata edit.
- Changing the compiler (`src/lib/arena/compile/*`) or the stored price programs.
- The other 132 stored rows: only re-check them after the change (acceptance below).

## Build

- `src/lib/arena/vm/activation-price-support.ts`: accept `moveTo` with `to: "under"` (and a `under` ref to a bound choice) in `activationPriceProblem`.
- `src/lib/arena/vm/activate.ts`: the payment path that charges a price (`canPayPriceProgram` and the payment itself) must place the chosen card under its target and count it as paid only when that move happened.
- `src/lib/arena/vm/actions.ts`: the 20-14-1 `unlessPay` caller takes the same answer, with no separate rule.
- `src/lib/arena/glossary.ts`: update the activation-price entry.
