Refs #907. Five printed Battle Cards share one Permanent: BT18-099 Tora, BT18-101 Fasha, BT18-103 Shugesh, BT18-105 Borgos (each “, Feelings Bequeathed”) and BT18-108 Bardock, Crew Leader.

> [Permanent] If your Leader is a ≪Bardock's Crew≫ card and there are 4 or more ≪Bardock's Crew≫ cards with different card names in your energy, Z-Energy, Battle Area, and/or Drop, this card gets +6000 power.

All five print the special traits Saiyan and Bardock's Crew. The count clause was unread, so the compiler kept only the Leader requirement and the +6000.

**Owner-agreed reading:** the count is four DISTINCT FULL CARD NAMES over the union of the own energy, Z-Energy, Battle Area and Drop. Four is the combined threshold, not four per area. Copies and other prints of one name count once; two names sharing a character both count. No Saiyan filter. The card counts itself when it is in a listed area. Hand, deck, Life, Warp and the opponent's areas do not count.

**Official sources, read 17 Oct 2026:** Bandai card list entries for all five cards (text above, traits Saiyan/Bardock's Crew); the card Q&A (`card_faq.php`) has no entry for any of them and none about “different card names”; the errata page (`errata-cards.php`) lists no erratum for them (its only BT18 entries are BT18-052 and BT18-089).

## What was wrong, and the fix

The language already had the pieces: the `differentNames` selector flag and a `count` condition over a selector with `areas` (#541, BT29-030). Two reading bugs hid the clause:

1. `parseTarget` read “in your energy, Z-Energy, Battle Area, and/or Drop” as the energy alone. The area-pair reader stops at the first comma, so a list of three or more areas kept one. A list reader (`AREA_LIST_RE`) now returns every area of an all-distinct list of known area words, and falls back to the old behaviour otherwise.
2. `splitDisjunction` cut the sentence at the “or” of “and/or”. It no longer does.

Both are shared: no card id, name or trait is mentioned. No new language node, schema or contract change.

To keep newly-readable text honest, `parseCountCondition` now refuses three wordings, and a compound condition containing one is refused whole (otherwise the Leader half was read on alone and the count vanished):

- “different **character** names” (BT18-107): not the same measure as card names.
- “and/or” inside the description of the cards (“blue and/or yellow cards”, “<Pan> cards and/or cards with <GT>…”): a union of two descriptions. These were read wrongly before (as either part alone, or as a count of 1).
- “a total of N” across a list of three or more areas (P-616's seven): the article took the number and the count became “at least 1”. P-616 is therefore **unread** now instead of wrongly read; fixing it properly is a separate issue.

## Evidence

`scripts/verify/bardocks-crew-915.ts` (registered in `verify-arena`): printed text, compile, stored-row JSON round-trip, then the real VM, both seats and all five cards. Three names fail, four and five succeed; the four names spread over energy, Z-Energy, Battle and Drop; four in each single area; copies and prints of one name; same character with different names; Saiyan-only cards and non-Saiyan Crew cards; hand, deck, Warp, Life and the opponent's four areas; wrong Leader, and the Leader changing; the bonus arriving and leaving as cards move, with no double count between listed areas; stacking with an unrelated +5000; a face-down card not counting; reload of state and rules from JSON.

Full-catalog readings (`arena-readings --unread`, public deckplanet feed, no database) before and after the change, 13,615 skills printed (the script reads the whole feed; the Masters-only count was not isolated): the five Permanents now read fully and the same count reads on BT18-102 and its sibling costs.
