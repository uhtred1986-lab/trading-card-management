## Goal and scope
Teach a first-time player enough Masters to make and explain their own decisions using the actual app. The introduction takes the full screen; the World Tournament Announcer then becomes a compact contextual guide. After two player turns including an attack and a defensive response, the player can disable guidance and finish the same short training match.

The existing Site is a prototype, not evidence of learning. Its passive opponent, invented Max character and introductory copy are starting points to improve. Preserve current game controls and information on phone/tablet/desktop.
