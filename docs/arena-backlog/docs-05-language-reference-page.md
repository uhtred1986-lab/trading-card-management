**Source:** `docs/arena-rules-language.md` §3 ("statements and conditions are not listed here and never will be: they are generated from `OP_SCHEMA` and `COND_SCHEMA`"); `/arena/rules/keywords` as the pattern of a page generated from the compiler's own tables; `EXPR_SCHEMA`, `SELECTOR_FIELDS`, `FILTER_FIELDS` in `lang/ast.ts`.

**Problem.** The doc deliberately does not list the statements; the workbench's chip editor shows them one at a time; the referee gets them as a legend. The person typing a rule in the text view has nowhere to look up "what can I write after THEN" as a whole.

**Build.** `/arena/rules/language`: every op and condition with its fields (name, type, required, enum values) and its `sentence` example; the selector parts and flags; every filter field and its printed-form words; the expression forms; the cost items; the trigger vocabulary from `validateRule`'s list (from the definition once Stage 3 lands); the literals table from the doc. Client-safe, generated at render from the schemas — no hand-written list. A link from the text view's error line (`expected[]`) to the matching row.

**Acceptance.** Gate; `npm test` checks the page's data source is exactly the schemas (a snapshot of the generated reference in `contract/fixtures/`, regenerated by `contract:emit`, so a schema change shows up as a reviewed diff).
