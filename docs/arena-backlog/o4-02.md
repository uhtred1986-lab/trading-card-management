## Delivered (13 Oct 2026, Refs #655 — not closed)

`src/lib/arena/local/pack.ts` is the portable half: the pack format (`OFFLINE_PACK_FORMAT = 1`, kind
`dbs-arena-offline-pack`), sealing, validation, qualification and instantiation through `browser.ts`.
The card payload is a #640 replay bundle, reused unchanged (definitions with both faces, programs `id`
and `id#back`, artwork references, ruleset files, interpreter revision). Around it the pack carries
the decklists with their `legality()` summary, generated/token dependencies, a transitive completeness
manifest (recomputed by validation, never trusted), compatibility (pack and bundle formats, interpreter
revision, VM state and snapshot contract versions), the missing inputs, the audit's blocker register
with issue links, and a `qualification` that validation recomputes. `scripts/build-arena-offline-pack.mts`
is the build-time half: it reads the #649 catalog snapshot, the repo rulesets and the compiler (no
database, network or provider) and builds one pack for the pair SD15 (A) / SD5 (B).

Proof: `scripts/verify/arena-offline-pack.mts` (`npm test`). Two builds are byte-identical; removing
a token card, a Leader's second face, a program, an art item or a ruleset file fails validation even
with every hash recomputed; tampering, a forged `playable`, an unsupported format or VM version, a
foreign interpreter and an unread program each keep the pack unplayable; the blocker register equals
the audit's; a validated pack instantiates both decks in both seats and plays; the field set is exact
(no account or collection field); the import graph of `local/pack.ts` reaches no database, catalog
source, provider, builtin or legacy engine (`offlinePackRule`).

**Open:** the built pack is **not playable**, by design: U1-U4, U6, U8 and the Deck A legality decision
are open, U7's specified costs for SD15-02 and DB3-001 are recorded as missing inputs (owner data,
`--specified-costs` takes them), and SD15-01's back and SD15-03 have unread clauses. Acceptance bullets
2 and 3 hold for the mechanism; the curated decks' playability stays with those blockers' owners. The
rules engine still reads printed costs through `compile.ts` helpers (`costIsOnlyOrbs`) at game time — a
pre-existing reach of the VM, not a compile of card text, left as it is.
