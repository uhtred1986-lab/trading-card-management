## Build
**Phase track** (`stage/ArenaStage.tsx` `arena-phases-row`, `stage/phone-board.module.css`):
- Show the phase row on phones again, as one row of about 30 px: Draw · Charge · Main · Battle · End.
- The current phase is filled in the turn colour; earlier phases are dimmed.
- The prompt panel's first line names the phase and whose turn it is ("Main phase · your turn", "Claude's turn · Battle phase").

**Player strips** (`stage/StageZones.tsx`):
- Life is a number with a "Life" label.
- Energy shows **⚡ ready/total** next to the chips.
- Claude's hand is drawn as face-down card backs (`--card-back`) with the count beside them, so the count is visibly Claude's. TinyFish read "HAND 4" as the player's own hand.
- Tapping a strip opens the deck/drop sheet, which includes the **decklist**.

**Leader vs Battle Card** (`stage/StageZones.tsx`, `stage/StageCard.tsx`):
- The Leader sits in its own bay, set off from the Battle Cards by a light dotted rule. Keep the LEADER tag.
