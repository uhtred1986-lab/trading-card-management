## Build
**Hand** (`stage/Hand.tsx`, `stage/phone-board.module.css`):
- Cards are 84 px wide (66 px while defending), with a 22 px cost disc that turns red when the card is unaffordable.
- A chip under each card says what it can do now: **▲ Play**, **▲ Play · 2 targets**, **▲ Play · skill idle**, **Need 4 ⚡**, **Counter**, **Charge**, and in Combo **+10k**. All of these are read from the snapshot (`legal`, abilities, costs).
- **More than 6 cards: the hand fans.** Every card stays on screen, overlapped, each showing its cost disc and a status dot (green playable · amber skill idle · red too expensive · grey counter). An **Open hand · N** button sits at the end of the fan. This is not a mode; it switches on by itself.
- Drag-to-play stays. A card that needs a second look opens its card view instead of being played.

**Card view** (`shared-sheets.tsx`, the review sheet; `ArenaCard.tsx`):
- **Your own cards** (hand or your board) open as the **big card**, then its actions directly below, then the card text.
  - Two layouts, chosen in the view and remembered: **Full card** (about 262 px wide on a 360 px phone) or **Top half** (header and art at full width).
- **Claude's cards** open as a **peek**: a compact sheet at most 56 % of the screen, small card beside the name, actions and full text, with the board and any target still in view. **⤢ Bigger** and **‹ Board** switch, and the choice is kept for your own cards.
- In the hand: a **filmstrip** of every hand card with status dots, ‹ › and swipe. Above 6 cards, add filters **All · Playable · Counters**. Paging controls are at least 44 px wide.
- Printed text stays as live text. Every art source we have is the same 260×362 scan, too soft to read skill text on a phone.

**Charge** (`stage/Hand.tsx`, `stage/PromptPanel.tsx`):
- Tapping a hand card selects it ("✓ Charge this"). The prompt asks **"Charge Super Saiyan Son Goku?"** with the energy change, **Charge it** and **Pass · don't charge**.
- Tap another card to compare; tap the chosen one again to read it. No popup covers the hand.
