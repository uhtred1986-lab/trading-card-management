# #549 remaining Auto action-price audit (12 October 2026)

This is a read-only follow-up to merged PR #692. Issue #549 remains open. The examples below are **synthetic compiler probes**, not claims about printed Auto cards or the full catalog. I passed each pre-colon phrase through `parseSkills` and `compileCostProgram` as `[Auto] <price>: When you play this card, draw 1 card.` with `DATABASE_URL=''`. `compile/prices.ts` deliberately feeds price text to the general effect reader (`compileAction`/`compileSkill`), so a readable `price.ops` is not proof that the VM can charge it. `auto-costs.ts::readableAutomaticPrice` is the separate payment boundary.

Manual 5-8-1 defines payment as carrying out the specified action. Sections 5-8-2 and 5-8-2-2 require **every** required part and the entire action to be performable; 5-8-2-2-1-1 requires the specified card to reach its named area despite replacement. Section 5-8-4 says a failed attempted payment does not activate the skill. Section 9-6-4 gives an Auto master a pay/decline choice for a necessary cost; 9-6-4-1-1 makes a satisfied condition compulsory, but does not make an accompanying action free. Thus an unsupported price must remain typed unknown, not be labeled unaffordable or paid.

| Priority | Compiler-derived price family and exact probe result | Current boundary and next proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0: supported grammar with costly search | The existing registered `BUDGET549` probe compiles `choose Battle → switchMode rest → choose three hand → moveTo drop → switchMode self rest`. A dormant conditional replacement on a 20-card hand keeps its normal replay and exceeds 2,000 visits. | `canFinishAutomaticPrice` has an exact failed-state cache and narrow sound capacity/dead-branch proofs, but its generic 2,000-visit `NotYet` is reachable. This remains the biggest supported-price blocker. A general fix must establish terminating state equivalence and useful negative-case latency with saved aliases, choice order, conditional replacements, source moves, and both choosers. Raising/removing the bound alone cannot establish that. Reviewed saved-alias negative and different-chooser direct-transfer cases are now decided and must not be cited as unknown. |
| P1: paid prefix, conditional tail | `Choose 1 card in your hand and place it in your Drop Area, then if you do, shuffle your deck` compiles `choose(c0) → moveTo(c0, drop) → if(chose(c0)) { shuffle }`. The same prefix followed by `if you have 3 or more life, switch this card to Rest Mode` compiles an `if(life >= 3) { switchMode(self, rest) }` with no `else`. | `readableAutomaticPrice` rejects every empty-else `if`, and nested `shuffle` is forbidden. Yet a mandatory completed prefix can pay the price on either branch, while an executed conditional action must also finish. The smallest **grammar** increment is path-sensitive proof of an already completed mandatory payment, then guarded conditional adjuncts. A shuffle may be admitted only when terminal on every path and after payment, with no later random-order-dependent choice. Do not infer that `if you do` is true merely from syntax; replay the condition against the paid state. No printed Auto using this shape is established. |
| P2: one-card visibility/face change | `Flip 1 card in your life face up` compiles `choose(life, count=1) → faceUp(c0)`. `Flip this card to its back side` compiles `flip(self)`. | Both are rejected by the whitelist. `script.ts` currently skips `faceUp` if the card is already in that state and skips `flip` for an already-flipped card, a card without a back, or a card outside Leader. A small **operation** increment could admit only the exact state-changing, valid-area single-card forms: require an eligible selected card before a prompt, verify the post-state after `setFaceUp`/`flip`, and retain the event and hidden-information behavior. Do not count a skipped operation as paid or admit a broad `faceUp/flip` op by name alone. These probes are synthetic. |
| P3: KO and actual play | `KO 1 of your Battle Cards` compiles `choose(battle) → ko(c0)`. `Play 1 Battle Card from your hand` compiles `choose(hand) → play(p0)`. | Both are rejected. KO has its own Indestructible/prohibition and replacement path and can complete without a Drop move only under specific rules; replacing it with `moveTo drop` would erase KO causes. `play` starts a nested play/cost/counter flow and cannot be equated to simple placement; `moveTo(to: play)` is also explicitly refused. Each needs a dedicated whole-action proof and saved continuation, not a whitelist addition. |
| P4: observation or generated-card actions | `Look at the top card of your deck`, `Draw 1 card`, `Add the top card of your deck to your life`, and `Add cards from your life to your hand until you have 6 life left` compile `look`, `draw`, `addLife`, and `lifeDownTo` respectively. | They are rejected. The normal interpreter may look at an empty deck, draw fewer than requested, or move with `reason: effect`; as a price each would need exact quantity/target/completion checks, correct cost cause and replacement handling, and private-information proof. A standalone look also changes no public resource, so its payment semantics should be grounded by a printed cost or ruling before broadening the validator. |

The current supported price set is `choose`, `discard`, `mill`, `switchMode`, `hidden`, `moveTo` except `to: play`, and `removeMarker`, plus mandatory public `reveal`, a top-level terminal shuffle after another payable step, and nonempty payable `if`/`chooseMode` branches. Strict host checks and isolated interpreter replay are part of that claim; the opcode name alone is never sufficient. A private look, a free/empty conditional or modal path, standalone or nonterminal shuffle, and an unread price remain explicit unknowns. The catalog feed failed DNS (`EAI_AGAIN api.deckplanet.net`) and real-deck fuzz 40 could not start without deck inputs, so this audit makes no reachability claim about printed Auto cards beyond the source-identified fixtures in `pv-r02.md`.

The next bounded implementation should start with the P1 paid-prefix conditional grammar because it closes a compiler-derived structural gap without adding a new effect opcode. If a printed Auto price is found in the P2 family first, the strict one-card `faceUp` payment is the smallest independent opcode addition. Neither increment discharges P0's general search proof or the owner-run catalog/fuzz acceptance.
