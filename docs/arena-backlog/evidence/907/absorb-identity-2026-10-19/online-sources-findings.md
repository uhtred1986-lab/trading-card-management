## Binding card text and authority

The fresh BT3-070 cardlist still shows the original “choose **up to** 1 each”
cost, and links directly to its errata. The official 2 March 2018 errata changes
that phrase to “choose **1 each**,” effective with the 9 March Series3 release.
The corrected catalog and applied record already use that mandatory cost.
Preserve that correction; the stale cardlist is no reason to relax payment or
repeat the #934 catalog repair. The green-orb image is `green_ball.png`.

The corrected skill is:

> [Union-Absorb] {g}, choose 1 each of <Android 14> and <Android 15> from your
> Drop Area and place them under this card: Choose up to 1 <Android 13> with an
> energy cost of 7 from your deck and play it on top of this card. Then, shuffle
> your deck.

Manual22-13-1 defines Union as Activate:Main; 22-13-6-1 defines Absorb as
“skill cost : effect”; 22-13-6-2 limits activation to the Battle Area. The source
is the in-play activating card. The named Drop cards pay its cost by going under
that source. The optional Deck card is selected by the later effect and played
on top of that same source (22-13-6-3). It is not a hand-activated Fusion/Potara
material procedure and has no equal-power requirement.

Cold Bloodlust FAQQ6 and Crusher Ball FAQQ5 confirm that cost is paid and Absorb
activated **before** the first Counter:Play window and before the Deck card is
chosen. A later window occurs after entry/shuffle. Preserve the delivered
payment/Counter/effect staging rather than preselecting the Deck target as a
material. BT3-070 FAQQ1 restricts search to necessary information; it does not
permit inspection of unrelated deck cards or Life.

Manual22-13-6-3-1 carries continuous position/power effects; failed entry sends
the selected incoming card to Drop, with the explicit return-to-hand replacement
exception (6-3-2/-2-1). These existing semantics remain separate from the missing
stored identity. No new mode, Counter, destination or cost interpretation is
introduced by this audit.

## Bounded proof/review edges

- Exact Main/Battle activation and acting controller; wrong area/timing must not
  acquire an offer from a misleading display tag.
- Mandatory distinct Android14 and Android15 from the printed Drop area, one
  green resource, placement under the activating source; missing/wrong material
  and wrong source must refuse before claiming activation.
- After payment/first Counter window, optional Android13 cost7 from Deck onto
  the same source. Declining the optional target retains paid costs and the
  printed shuffle. Existing source/host invalidation and saved epoch guards must
  prevent replacement copies or another Android13 from silently becoming host.
- JSON-restored offers, prices and continuations require the typed identity;
  missing/malformed contracts cannot fall back to displayed Union variant.
- Preserve the actual v9 record's accepted cost/effect/readings, usage/validity
  and draft status. Broader Absorb substitutions, alternate source permissions,
  other printed costs and granted variants are not proved by this single row.

These are review targets, not claims that new scenarios have already passed.
The existing material/payment receipts are prior evidence and must stay intact.

## Potara feasibility, outside the primary selection

BT2-012/front/0 is a small known printed reference: fresh official cardlist shows
Union-Potara {5}, SonGoku and Vegeta, Active entry on the two specified cards.
The existing #927 fixture proves its basic semantic shape, unequal-power
materials, stacking and saved choices. Manual22-13-5 requires hand activation,
payment and one of each specified character in Battle, then entry on both in
any order; only power effects carry. This differs from Absorb's Battle source
and printed effect-driven incoming target.

There is **no BT2-012 actual record in the current eleven-row export**. Existing
#927 extension fixtures also retain expressly labelled owner guidance for stack
order/carryover details. Therefore Potara is a plausible next bounded contract,
not a free addition to this actual-row authority claim. Freeze its exact current
catalog/row and explicit scope before including it in the same PR; do not extend
to all51 Potara activations or all energy/Warp/under-Unison/opponent-area
permissions by inference. No new ruling is requested or supplied here.
