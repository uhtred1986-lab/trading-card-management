# AI presentation fixtures (#601)

This fixture replaces the rules-versus-legacy comparison in `scripts/verify/ai-vm.ts`. It exercises only the rules engine. The provider is a local fake; the HTTP-wire cases use an injected `fetch` stub. No database, external provider, or paid request is used.

The state fixture is reproducible from the checked-in test definitions: two 50-card decks (`V1` and `V-BLUE`) with `L-RED` and `L-BLUE`, seed `7`, first player `p1`, both mulligans declined, one Charge Phase and Main Phase for each player, then p1's turn-3 charge. The test relabels the first matching card left in each deck and stages these known cards: p1's CRIT in battle, two V1 in energy; p2's BLOCKER in battle and hand, one V-BLUE in energy and one in Drop. It turns p1's first life card face up (3-9-2-1). The JSON fixture pins the ordered instance IDs and card/mode/visibility for every zone, the turn and prompt, and legal actions. The text fixtures pin both viewers' rendered state, decklists, combo question and system primer.

The zone IDs are generated by the deterministic seed-7 setup. Their expected areas and card identities are independently checked against the setup sequence, card definitions and raw fixture staging: each player has one Leader, eight life cards and a 50-card main deck; the fixture's moves from deck into battle/energy/hand/Drop account for the stated zone totals. The seven p1 V1 hand cards each have cost 1 against two active Red energy; CRIT costs 3 and cannot be played. The exact turn-3 menu therefore contains seven distinct V1 plays, End turn, and attacks by the Leader (10,000) and CRIT (15,000) on the opposing Leader (10,000). On the blocker prompt, the only choices are p2#44 or declining. The Offense Step menu is seven V1 combos, one CRIT combo, or Pass. Face-up life and displayed power/keywords are checked against the fixture definitions and the manual rules cited in the source test.
