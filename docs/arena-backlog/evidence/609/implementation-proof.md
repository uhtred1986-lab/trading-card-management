# #609 battle-cleanup checkpoints: implementation proof

The rules VM now separates the DBS manual's battle cleanup into 8-5-4, 8-5-7, and 8-5-10 checkpoints. `vm/battle.ts` persists the stage and subject cursors before announcing or moving each card, so a pending Auto, choice, or JSON reload resumes without replaying an occurrence. The generic Combo-to-own-Drop trigger is declared in `rulesets/dbs/triggers.rules` and read from exact synthetic wording by `text/triggers.ts`. A matching Battle Area source answers for each qualifying Combo card at 8-5-6. The normal checkpoint runner then resolves those Autos at 8-5-7, before any 8-5-8 physical departure. That later move suppresses only the already-announced generic trigger; other movement Autos remain eligible.

At 8-5-6 the ordinary same-player subject's requested card information is projected in Drop under §9-6-9-1-3 and saved on the pending record/program frame. The live card stays in Combo through 8-5-7, so selectors, costs, and live zone tests still read Combo. Source validity and negation are evaluated when the trigger is fulfilled. The destination-owner/source-holder split is checked before the printed “your Combo ... your Drop” trigger qualifies. Tokens do not supply that Drop destination. A replacement that changes a pre-announced subject's destination has no established projected Drop information; the VM raises a typed #609 interruption rather than inventing it. A replacement with no qualifying generic watcher continues through its ordinary route.

At 8-5-8, the VM moves only cards still in Combo and records which actually reached Drop. At 8-5-9 it announces `battleEnd` and the special §8-5-10-1 “after you combo with this card” Autos for those actual Combo-to-Drop departures. Captured hand-entry provenance remains through the synthetic 8-5-6 notice and the real 8-5-8 move; it is used once for the hand-qualified special Auto at 8-5-9. The runner drains the second group at 8-5-10 before the existing end-of-battle effects and return to Main.

`scripts/verify/battle-cleanup-609.ts` proves the actual 8-5-7 and 8-5-10 pauses, JSON reload at both checkpoints, projected subject information versus live Combo selection, both players' Autos, simultaneous occurrences and order selection, one physical departure per remaining card, no repeat on continuation, source departure after activation, source negation before trigger, private Snapshot projection, token and controlled-card exclusions, the [Ultimate] replacement boundary, and the separately timed hand-origin special Auto. The existing #551 Z-Energy, #672 Combo-entry, zero-power, Heroic, and #683 suites remain controls. `old-main-cleanup-pause.json` was generated on untouched `2f04b7e` `origin/main` during the former grouped `cleanup` stage. The focused proof resumes it under the changed VM and verifies that its already-completed moves and battle-end pending group do not replay. If an invalid legacy frame tries to re-enter that native cleanup work, it gets a typed #609 interruption instead of a guessed migration.

There is no real card with the generic 8-5-6 wording in the repository catalog or fixtures. The focused generic proof uses clearly synthetic text and operations; the existing `COMBOBACK` case is a distinct special §8-5-10-1 wording. The #551 fixture includes a non-Battle Extra Card remaining in Combo and expects it to leave for Drop during cleanup. This behavior remains as a compatibility fixture, while the literal §8-5-8 instruction names Battle Cards. Neither the generic fixture nor that Extra Card boundary establishes a new printed-card ruling. The legacy engine was not changed.
