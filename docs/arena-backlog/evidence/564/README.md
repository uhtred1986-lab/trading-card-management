The focused script asserts one mounted `PromptPanel`, no rendered Match column, browser hit testing for Pass, target, Counter card, pinned Senzu activation and final payment, and no horizontal overflow. It also asserts:

- Main: the wrong-window Senzu tap sends nothing; a one-red-energy Battle Card play sends the exact legal action, moves the card into battle, emits a mode change and rests exactly one ready energy.
- Counter: Whis sends the exact legal Counter, emits `attackNegated`, rests the one ready Blue energy and produces the engine's next choice. A stale poll cannot roll the decision back; a reload reconstructs the newer revision.
- Target: tapping the attacking card as an invalid target cancels selection without sending an action; the valid opposing leader sends the exact legal attack and emits the engine attack event.
- Battle/PayCost: keyboard Pass emits `powerCompare`; keyboard marker payment emits `energyMarker` and a combo move, and retains both ready energy cards. The cost previews are the snapshot's own payment options.

The reference [grounded desktop draft](../../../arena-redesign/playability-abilities-desktop.jpg) uses a single right action/selection region. This implementation keeps the existing card inspector and In play tabs below the only decision panel, preserving the app's board controls and EngineAPI wording. Log, settings and history remain available through existing on-demand controls. Synthetic fixture cards deliberately differ from the draft's staged catalogue scene.
