## Fixed decision criteria and reporting

Retain the existing minimum 160 curated paired deals, at least 20 decisive pairs
(won-both/lost-both) and a 95% Wilson lower bound above 50% on won-both among
those decisive pairs. Any demonstrated cell regression (cell upper bound below
50%) fails the statistical gate. Cells with fewer than 20 decisive pairs remain
explicitly underpowered; a pooled advantage cannot certify all archetypes.
All scheduled games must finish with zero illegal answers, execution errors or
privacy failures or cycle-guard takeovers. Draw-containing and unfinished pairs remain separate and all
attempts stay in the denominator. Crashes have no invented move/result metrics.
Report per-cell, per-archetype and per-seat intervals plus all consumed seeds.

Independent promotion requirements remain: Worker whole-step p95 <=30 ms,
maximum <=300 ms and retained heap <=16 MiB; policy-only p95 <=5 ms. Shared-host
Node timings are descriptive and cannot satisfy these Worker gates. There is
no automatic default or difficulty promotion. A negative or inconclusive result
is retained as the completed experiment, with limitations stated.

No stopping or extending for significance; no candidate tuning after the first
holdout attempt. Source/fixture/protocol changes invalidate resume. Durable
private journals retain every attempted outcome and permit only disjoint fixed
shards. Interrupted/in-flight seeds are consumed and never relabeled fresh.
Hidden twins sample both policies at a fixed interval, use only masked inputs,
and must preserve the answer and its legality. No raw private state is imported.

The existing six defensive variants and four tactical cases remain focused
semantic controls. Known unaffordable-defense combo waste remains a named
limitation: spending one combo can delay loss without preserving the defender
through both attacks. This experiment does not repair it. New card/mechanic
failures get a reproducer and owner rather than silent policy/rule changes.
