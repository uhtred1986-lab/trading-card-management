## Outcome

Freeze a presentation-only contract before other workers touch runtime integration. Existing numbered
public beats remain authoritative.

## Acceptance

- Attacker buildup/release, actual defender/blocker reaction, negate, repel, damage and KO are distinct
  supported cues; hit/KO are never inferred from an attack declaration.
- Character/face mapping, technique override, neutral fallback reason, cue start/stop markers and the
  consumed event key are represented. Current types are extended; no parallel playback engine.
- One character-action route for leader and Battle Cards; actual source/destination card motion for
  play/Drop/Warp/Swap/Union stays separate from combat staging, without inventing material stacks.
- Events absent from the public snapshot stay unsupported, never guessed. Hidden cards reveal no
  identity through artwork, voices, prompt metadata or asset fetches.
- Timings are presentation-relative and bounded by the beat's duration; clip length cannot extend a
  turn or wait for media.
- Small public fixtures for attack→hit, block, negate, repel, hidden source and cancelled sequence, with
  minimal adapter assertions.

## Delivered

`ActionSequence`, `ACTION_CUE_IDS`, `CueMarker`, `NeutralReason` in `src/lib/arena/immersion/types.ts`;
`actionCues`, `actionSequence`, `cueMarkers`, `CUE_WINDOW_MS`, `SYNTH_FALLBACK` in `events.ts`
(`soundCue` now derives from the contract with identical tones); fixtures in `action-fixtures.ts`;
assertions in `scripts/verify/arena-immersion.mts` (now in the `npm test` chain). The banners and
`audio.ts` are unchanged: wiring the sequence into them is #1105/#1109.
