## Union as the first reference family

The bounded #907 typed-payment evidence slice for BT11-013 Fusion and BT3-070
Absorb is tracked in [typed-payment-907.md](evidence/923/typed-payment-907.md).
It records prior focused proof separately from new typed-record acceptance and
stored-rule application.

Owner feedback on 19 October: the different Union variants were a major source of repeated difficulty. Use the existing implementations to establish three explicit contracts; do not funnel them through a generic text pattern or rebuild already accepted variant behaviour.

| Contract | Variant-specific responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Fusion | Named/equal-power material selection, permitted source areas, material/payment order and entry |
| Potara | Named material set, source-extension transfer rules, stack order and carryover |
| Absorb | Printed cost/effect on the in-play source, target/onto binding and entry |
| Shared machinery | Typed selection, payment, permission/restriction modifiers, Counter/replacement handling, events and persisted choices |

Support cards modify typed permissions, costs, material constraints or triggers. They must not create another hidden English interpretation. Keep normal play separately available when legal.

The matrix pairs every supported modifier with positive and negative cases on its applicable variant, and an unaffected-variant control. Run it through persisted/reloaded rules, including payment failure, Counter outcome and source/host invalidation. #907 owns the incremental authoritative-record changes; this issue owns the Union evidence matrix.
