Owner requested a complete Union audit after reviewing the three Gogeta Warp-material Permanents. Refs #907. This issue tracks shared runtime semantics and all catalog Union references, rather than assuming a clean parse proves playability.

Source: official comprehensive rules 22-13 (all subsections), https://www.dbs-cardgame.com/pdf/rulemanual.pdf?ver_4.00 ; https://www.dbs-cardgame.com/us-en/rule/card_faq.php ; https://www.dbs-cardgame.com/us-en/rule/errata-cards.php ; https://www.dbs-cardgame.com/us-en/rule/keyword-skills.php . Local manual: docs/rules/rulemanual.txt.

Read-only 15 October snapshot: 6,552 Masters cards. 229 cards / 233 faces mention Union. 268 parsed records reference it; 170 actual top-level Union activations (63 Fusion, 51 Potara, 56 Absorb). Two Potara lines are secondary tags beside other keywords and must remain in the inventory. Two Absorb activation records contain unread clauses; many surrounding support/permission/cost/trigger effects are also unread. Counts measure syntax, not complete runtime support.

Rules: Fusion activates from hand, reveals one of each specified character with equal power, sends materials to Drop as cost before additional skill cost, and plays the fusion card. Potara activates from hand, chooses specified characters in Battle, stacks onto both (chosen order), and carries continuous power effects. Expanded Potara sources first move to Battle without area-movement triggers/counter timing. Absorb activates from Battle and follows its printed cost/effect to play the specified card onto the source, carrying position/power effects. All three have explicit failed-entry Drop / return-to-hand exceptions.

Acceptance:
- [ ] Check in complete public card/face/skill inventory, exact text, parser results, unread clauses, and coverage matrix for all three variants plus support references.
- [ ] Audit activation areas, exact named character set and filters, equal power where applicable, material/additional cost order, costs and source permissions, chosen destinations/stack order, carryover, triggers, Counter: Play windows, failed-entry handling, serialized choices and hidden information.
- [ ] Link focused issues for each verified shared gap; distinguish unread, known wrong, supported with proof, and unproven.
- [ ] Review official FAQ/errata and document applied catalog errata and unanswered/absent card-specific rulings.
- [ ] Retain per-family closure evidence; do not close because 168 of 170 activation records compile without unread clauses.

Initial verified gaps: plain-hand Fusion chooser lacks namedSet validation (glossary explicitly admits duplicate same-character selections); Potara chooses its second material after playing onto the first and needs exact-set/order/failure/carryover review; Warp Fusion self-permissions and several Potara/Absorb source extensions unread. Focused issues will be linked as the audit is completed.

Owner clarification: Union is an alternative skill play, not a mandatory replacement for normal play. Unless another printed restriction forbids it, normal energy-cost play from hand remains available without Fusion materials. P-286 normal cost6 versus Union-Fusion cost3 plus qualifying equal-power materials; verify both routes and restrictions. The glossary must explain Fusion, Potara and Absorb separately.
