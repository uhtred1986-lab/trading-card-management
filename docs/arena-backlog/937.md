Refs #907. Priority P1: an existing Leader is unable to attack at all because a target restriction becomes a blanket attack prohibition.

Both faces of BT1-057 (Broly / Broly, The Legendary Super Saiyan, Galactic Battle) print “[Permanent] This card cannot attack Battle Cards.” Existing compiler-owned draft records instead contain unconditional `forbid what:attack`, and the current VM asks attack prohibitions without the prospective defender. This clean parse incorrectly blocks attacks against the opponent's Leader.

Official Bandai card FAQ for **both faces**, Q1, explicitly allows an attack against the opponent's Leader and allows the opponent to redirect it using a Battle Card's Blocker. Therefore distinguish choosing a forbidden initial attack target from a later legal redirection. Do not re-check this declaration restriction at Blocker resolution.

Acceptance:
- [ ] Inventory target-qualified attack prohibitions; preserve a semantic defender filter in shared compiler/program support, not a Broly ID branch. Unknown or unrepresentable qualifiers must not silently become blanket prohibitions.
- [ ] Both faces and both seats: actual stored-rule fixture → legal action menu → applied action shows an opponent-Leader attack allowed and a direct attack against an otherwise legal rested opponent Battle Card refused.
- [ ] A legal opponent-Leader attack may subsequently be redirected by Blocker, as the official Q&A requires.
- [ ] Defend direct forged actions and stale target bindings; missing/invalid defender context must not bypass a target restriction. Preserve ordinary “can't attack” prohibitions, attack taxes, mode/timing checks, defender immunity, and other existing attack restrictions.
- [ ] Review full compiler corpus deltas; update glossary and any changed language/contract fixtures; pass typecheck, lint, full tests and production build.
- [ ] No live record writes. Preserve owner/Claude records. Root can separately review a guarded refresh of affected compiler-owned records after deployment.
