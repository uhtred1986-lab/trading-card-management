# White Cell: rules proof and strategy benchmarks

This plan uses BT31-116 and its actual printed supporting cards in the saved Masters catalog, checked against the current test sources. **Deck 29 being ready is qualification of its stored programs/inputs, not proof of all its mechanics or good AI play.** No tests or paid calls were run for this review. “Existing” below means assertions were inspected; “partial” means the engine family is exercised with synthetic cards or only a reading assertion; “missing” means no named complete real-card scenario was found in the searched suites. This is a bounded inventory, not a fresh passing-test result.

## Exact printed anchors

- **BT31-116 front, Cell:** “You can't include non-white Extra in your deck.” Main once per turn: “Place up to 1 white <Cell> card from your deck into its owner's Drop, then shuffle your deck.” Awaken: life 4 or less **or** a total of at least 3 white Cell cards and Extras in Drop; “Draw 2 cards, then add cards from your life to your hand until you have 6 life left.”
- **BT31-116 back, Cell, Perfect Power:** “Switch 1 of your energy to Hidden Mode: When your <Cell> or <Cell Jr.> card -- both white and with an energy cost of 3 or more -- is played, activate an [Activate: Main/Battle] skill of up to 1 mono-white Extra with an energy cost of 2 in your hand.” Back Main/Battle once per turn recovers up to 1 white cost-2 Extra with that skill from Drop to hand.
- **BT31-119, Cell, Creeping Terror:** “If you have energy in Hidden Mode, reduce the energy cost of this card in your Z-Deck by 2.” On play, place a total of up to 2 white Cell cards or white Extras from deck into Drop, shuffle, then place up to 1 white Android from Drop under this card.
- **BT31-129, Cell, Perfect Form:** Deflect/Dual Attack. “[EX-Evolve][Limit 1](White), if you have 3 or more energy and you draw 1 card: White <Cell> card with an energy cost of 3 or more.” Attacking reveals up to 1 energy. Removal from Battle Area **by a skill** plays up to 1 white cost-4 Cell from Drop.
- **BT31-130, Cell, Absorbed Form:** Blocker. Under a white Cell, while your Leader is a white Cell, the top card gains Barrier. “[Auto][Limit 1]{1}: When this card is removed from your Battle Area by a skill,” all of both players' cost-4-or-less Battle Cards are placed into their owners' Drop.
- **BT31-131, Cell, Ominous Android:** Main/Battle Limit 1: white Cell Leader, at least 2 energy, switch 1 energy Hidden; play this card from Drop in **Rest Mode**. Battle Limit 1: pay {1}, opponent has at least 2 energy, opponent's turn; play up to 1 white cost-4 Cell from Drop on top of this card in **Active Mode**.
- **BT31-133, Cell Jr., the Fiendish:** On play draw 1, then add 1 hand card to Z-Energy, skills negated for the turn. Battle: white Leader, opponent at least 2 energy, opponent's turn, switch 1 energy Revealed; play from hand, then up to 1 of your cards gets +5000 for the battle.
- **BT31-138, Cell's Solar Flare:** return up to 1 of your white Cell cards to hand; negate up to 1 keyword skill on an opponent Battle Card or Unison for the turn.
- **BT31-139, Absorb and Power Up:** draw 1; choose 1 white Cell Battle Card; optionally place 1 Android from Drop under it. **If you do**, play up to 1 white cost-4-or-less Cell from Drop on top of that chosen card in Active Mode.
- **BT31-140, Regeneration Skill:** draw 1, reveal up to 3 energy; additionally, when the opponent's card is attacking, your Leader gets +10000 for the battle.
- **BT31-141, Cell Jr. Summon:** play 2 Cell Jr. Tokens, with Blocker for the turn; printed tokens are 10000 power, combo cost 0, combo power 5000.
- **BT31-142, Cell's Kamehameha:** draw 1, up to 1 white Battle Card gets +10000 for the **turn**; additionally, on your turn, up to 1 opponent Battle Card is placed into its owner's Drop.
- **BT31-128, Cell, Ultimate Form:** Evolve {1}, at least 3 energy, white Cell base. On play draw 1, switch this card Active, optionally KO an opponent Battle Card, then **at end of turn** reveal all your energy. Its separate Main Limit 1 play costs {2}, requires white/Android Leader and at least 3 energy.

Manual basis: `docs/rules/rulemanual.txt` 5-3/5-8 payment; 5-5 skill play; 9-6 event occurrence/activation; 12-2-4 chosen Extra activation; 22-5 evolution; 22-11 once/limit; 23-5 Hidden Mode. In particular, Hidden cards have no front-side color/information (23-5-2), and switching Hidden/Revealed **does not change Active/Rest** (23-5-3). A revealed Rested energy is not refreshed energy. For any uncertainty beyond these anchors, record a Q&A/owner ruling before making an expected outcome a test.

Feed policies only the normal player-restricted observation: public board/resources/visible faces, the acting player's allowed hand, legal menu, battle stakes and previously observed history. Do not expose opponent hand/deck/Life identities, concealed energy faces, raw IDs, engine frames or referee context. Permuting unseen identities must not change an answer. Score acceptable action sets and achieved resource/battle outcomes; do not enforce one arbitrary card when equivalent choices exist.
