## Mechanisms built

- **Face-up life cards** (3-9-2-1), ~95 cards: one flag on the instance, a
  filter that reads "face-up" anywhere, both printed trigger wordings, and the
  colour qualifier they all print, checked where the flipping card is known.
- **Paying for a [Counter] with something other than energy** (5-3): `altCost`
  carries a compiled program, charged through the flow because most of those
  prices need the player to pick a card.
- **An offer the opponent declines** (20-16): `may` gained a chooser, so "your
  opponent may … / if they don't …" reads their answer.
- **Standing prohibitions on a card's own play** (20-14), read wherever the
  card is, because the moment that matters is a skill reaching into the Drop.
- **Fifteen new trigger moments**, each pended for the side whose wording it is.

## Correctness fixes, not features

These were the valuable half, and none of them appeared in any gap report —
a clause that compiles *wrongly* is invisible to every measure in the project.

- "At the end of **your** turn" fired at the end of both turns (164 cards), and
  116 skills that merely *mention* a turn boundary mid-effect were triggered by
  it. One "draw 1 card" drew five, because the End Phase re-offered it.
- Every duration a [Counter] created lasted a whole turn too long.
- A digit inside a name was read as a count: "your ≪Universe 6≫ cards" was six
  of them. 533 skills on 436 cards name something with a digit.
- Several colours in one description meant *all* of them, so "blue, yellow
  ≪Universe 6≫ cards" matched nothing at all.
- "In your opponent's Drop" resolved to the Battle Area; `non-black` and
  `non-[Super Combo]` were dropped entirely, widening the selection.
- `splitClauses` cut sentences into nonsense twice over — the ―…― aside and
  comma-separated lists — costing 98 skills a one-word fragment.
- A [Union-Fusion] prompt was unanswerable, caught by the fuzzer the day a deck
  with BT6-001 was added.
